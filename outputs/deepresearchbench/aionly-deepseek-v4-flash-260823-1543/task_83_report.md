# Comprehensive Research Report: Tablet-Style Devices for Payments and SaaS Applications

## Executive Summary

The global tablet POS systems market was valued at approximately **$8.5 billion in 2024** and is projected to reach **$30 billion by 2034**, growing at a CAGR of **13.4%** [1]. North America currently holds **35.7%** of global market share, while Asia-Pacific is the fastest-growing region. The market is characterized by a shift from traditional countertop terminals to Android-based smart terminals, increasing adoption of cloud-based POS (64% of installations), and the rise of SoftPOS technology that converts NFC-enabled smartphones into payment terminals [1][2].

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of tablet-style payment devices, covering manufacturers, hardware configurations, use cases, and regional market dynamics across North America, Japan/Korea, Southeast Asia, and South America.

## 4. Key Industry Trends

### 4.1 SoftPOS Technology
- Converts NFC-enabled Android phones into payment terminals, eliminating extra hardware costs
- Ideal for micro-merchants and mobile businesses
- Visa reports contactless payments represent over 70% of in-store transactions in several global markets [2]
- GMO Financial Gate and Soft Space alliance secured Japan's first MPoC certification [25]

### 4.2 Android Dominance
- Android dominates the smart POS landscape, offering lower device costs, more manufacturer choices, broader accessory compatibility, and greater flexibility compared to iPad-based systems [2]
- **Top five vendors** (Ingenico, VeriFone, PAX Technology, Newland Payment, Clover Network/Fiserv) accounted for **48.3%** of global Smart POS hardware revenues in 2025 [4]
- Global installed base of Smart POS terminals reached approximately **215 million units in 2025**, up from approximately 88 million in 2020 [4]

### 4.3 Cloud-Based POS
- **64%** of POS installations are now cloud-based [1]
- 67% of POS installations are now cloud-based [1]
- Cloud-connected share in South Korea expected to increase from **43% (2025) to 71% (2031)** [31]
- Cloud POS software market valued at **$7.1B in 2025** [4]

### 4.4 QR Code Payment Dominance (Southeast Asia)
- QR payments require only a smartphone and linked bank account or e-wallet (more inclusive than cards) [38]
- QR payments have lower or no fees compared to card MDR and require no POS terminals — just a printed QR code [38]
- Most ASEAN countries have national QR standards: KHQR (Cambodia), PromptPay (Thailand), QRIS (Indonesia), VietQR (Vietnam), DuitNow (Malaysia), QR Ph (Philippines) [38]
- QR is dominant in Cambodia, Thailand, and Indonesia where POS terminals are less common [38]

### 4.5 AI and Analytics Integration
- **73%** of retailers using generative AI report faster decision-making and enhanced automation [1]
- **ToastIQ**: AI-based intelligence engine for restaurants delivering personalized recommendations, timely prompts, and automated workflows [43]
- Companies using analytics see **23% stronger profitability** (Harvard Business Review) [1]

### 4.6 Biometric Authentication
- Fingerprint and NFC login features (Clover Station Duo, PAX devices)
- PopID and Toshiba partnership for biometrics at POS in South Korea [33]
- Biometrics is the fastest-growing sub-category at **24.3% CAGR** globally [4]
