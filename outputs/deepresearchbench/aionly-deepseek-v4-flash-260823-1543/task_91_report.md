# The Complete Saint Seiya Franchise Analysis: Armor Classes, Warriors, Power Levels, Techniques, and Fates

## Introduction

*Saint Seiya* (聖闘士星矢, *Seinto Seiya*, also known as *Knights of the Zodiac*) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masami Kurumada, serialized in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* from 1985 to 1990 and collected in 28 volumes. The franchise follows five mystical warriors — the Saints — who fight wearing sacred armors called **Cloths**, whose designs derive from the constellations they serve. The Saints defend the reincarnation of the goddess Athena against other gods (Poseidon, Hades, and others) seeking to dominate Earth [1]. The original manga spawned a 114-episode TV anime (1986–1989), a 31-episode Hades OVA trilogy (2002–2008), five theatrical films, and numerous spin-offs including *The Lost Canvas*, *Next Dimension*, *Omega*, and *Soul of Gold* [1].

This report is structured around the franchise's central organizing principle: the different **classes of armor** and the **categories of warriors** who wear them. For each significant character, it details power level, signature techniques (with Japanese names where relevant), key story arcs, and final fate, noting differences between the manga and the various anime adaptations where they exist.

## Part I: The Armor Classes of the Saint Seiya Universe

### 1. The Foundation: Cosmo, the Senses, and the Creation of the Cloths

**Cosmo** is the mystical energy derived from the Big Bang that powers all supernatural feats in the franchise. It is heavily influenced by emotion; burning it allows feats like pulverizing rocks and striking faster than sound. The "Supreme Seventh Sense" represents the maximum extension of human cosmo, generating a small-scale Big Bang, while the "Big Will" is the divine Cosmos of the gods, granting infinite power [3].

**The Senses** define the power hierarchy of Saints:
- **The Sixth Sense (Intuition)** — inherent to all cosmo-awakened fighters; enables telekinesis, teleportation, mind reading, and atomic destruction.
- **The Seventh Sense (Miraculosity)** — gives access to the essence of cosmo, enabling "miracles" like light-speed movement and generating absolute zero; it separates Gold Saints from all others.
- **The Eighth Sense (Arayashiki)** — allows the dead to function in the Underworld and can be achieved while alive.
- **The Ninth Sense (Godhood/Amalashiki)** — separates gods from mortals; grants immortality, resurrection, time control, and planetary control [3].

Athena has **88 Saints** divided into three ranks: **12 Gold Saints** (wearing Gold Cloths of the zodiac), **24 Silver Saints**, and **48 Bronze Saints** [3][4]. A core rule of the series is that "the power of a Saint is not related to the Cloth he wears but to his Cosmo" [3].

**Creation and materials.** Cloths were created during the Myth Age by the alchemists of the lost continent of Mu (Moo/Lemuria) to counter the power of Poseidon's Marine generals, who were impossible to defeat by unarmed followers of Athena [7]. They are forged from an ultra-rare celestial ore called **Orichalcum** mixed with **Gamanium** and **Stardust Sand**, infused with the energy of the universe itself; some contain the blood of gods [2][7]. Cloths are living entities that can evolve, repair themselves, and choose their wearers based on resonance with Cosmo. They can "die" if heavily damaged, and can only be revived with a Saint's blood (or blood from someone of equivalent strength); they are useless without a wearer burning their own Cosmo [7]. The Cloth repair technique (聖衣クロスの修復技術) is a remnant of the Muvian alchemist clan's art, practiced in the present era by the Jamir tribe, most notably by Aries Mu and his pupil Kiki [31].

**Rank-based durability and speed tiers** (approximate, as cited in fan analyses):
- **Bronze Cloths** — destroyed at ~4,000 kg of pressure or below −150°C; wearers strike at sound speed (Mach 1).
- **Silver Cloths** — freeze at −200°C; wearers move at Mach 2–5.
- **Gold Cloths** — freeze only at Absolute Zero (−273.15°C); wearers fight at light speed [3][4].

### 2. The Cloth Hierarchy: Bronze, Silver, and Gold

**Bronze Cloths.** The lowest tier, worn by the most numerous rank (48 Saints). Despite being weakest, they allow users to break the sound barrier, shatter mountains, and survive devastating attacks. The five main protagonists — Pegasus Seiya, Dragon Shiryu, Cygnus Hyoga, Andromeda Shun, and Phoenix Ikki — are Bronze Saints who transcend their rank through determination [2][4]. The **Phoenix Cloth** is a special case: it is the strongest Bronze Cloth ever made, possessing the remarkable ability to regenerate itself from ashes no matter how many times it is destroyed [2]. Notably, the Hypermyth recounts that a wicked man once forced the Phoenix Cloth upon himself; the armor detonated, destroying the Mu continent [8].

**Silver Cloths.** The mid-tier armors, offering superior protection and speed (Mach 2–5), and often granting unique abilities such as elemental powers or enhanced sensory perception. Silver Saints are frequently portrayed in the original series as ruthless, sadistic antagonists — "acting more like assassins than warriors of peace" — though some, like Eagle Marin and Ophiuchus Shaina, consistently assist the protagonists [5]. A few Silver Saints are explicitly noted to exceed Gold Saint level: **Lyra Orphée** (the "Legendary Saint"), **Crateris Suikyō** (described by Ikki as stronger than any Gold Saint he had fought), and **Altar Hakurei** (as strong as Grand Pope Sage) [5].

**Gold Cloths.** The pinnacle of Athena's armor, representing the twelve zodiac constellations. Because those constellations lie along the ecliptic, the Gold Cloths have absorbed the light of the sun since ancient times; when all twelve gather, they emit light and produce a resonant sound [4]. Gold Cloths are nearly indestructible, can withstand the power equivalent to a Big Bang, and have survived since the ages of myth; only the strength of a god in his true body can completely destroy one [4]. They can also move on their own or abandon morally corrupt wearers (as the Cancer Cloth abandoned Deathmask). The **Libra Cloth** uniquely contains six pairs of golden weapons (tonfa, spear, sword, shield, triple rods, twin rods) stated to be able to pulverize a star, used at the Libra Saint's discretion [4]. A battle between two equally matched Gold Saints results in a "Thousand Day War" stalemate [4]. Three united Gold Saints can unleash the forbidden **Athena Exclamation (アテナ・エクスクラメーション)**, releasing energy comparable to a small Big Bang; Athena banned it as a coward's technique [4]. All twelve Gold Saints together can perform the "Sacrifice of the Gold Saints," focusing their combined cosmo into Sagittarius' arrow — an attack more powerful than the Athena Exclamation, used to break through the Wailing Wall in Hell [4][33].

### 3. Black Cloths (Black Saints of Death Queen Island)

Black Cloths are the weapons of the renegade **Black Saints**, identical in appearance to legitimate Cloths but discovered in large quantities on Death Queen Island, a ruin site of the submerged Mu Continent. Around 1500 AD, rebel alchemists discovered the island and began crafting imitation Cloths; the most copied was the Phoenix Cloth — the only real Sacred Cloth remaining on the island. The imitations were weaker than originals but still powerful enough to concern the Sanctuary. The Sanctuary sealed the island and sent a keeper (Ikki's master Guilty) to suppress the Black Saints; when Ikki killed him and broke his mask, the seal was broken [8][9]. The Hypermyth adds that Athena created black Saint armor as a "backup," and that men who wore them sold their souls to demons and were banished to Death Queen Island, trapped in perpetual slaughter [8]. Notable Black Saints include the **Black Four Kings** (Black Pegasus, Black Cygnus, Black Dragon, Black Andromeda) and the anime-original **Black Phoenix**; the Lost Canvas features a separate faction led by Avid (Black Ara) [8][9].

### 4. Steel Cloths (Steel Saints)

The **Steel Saints** are **anime-only characters** (from the original 1986 anime; filler not present in Kurumada's manga, reportedly included at Bandai's insistence for toy sales). They were a top-secret technological task force created by Mitsumasa Kido (Athena's adoptive grandfather) through the Graad Foundation, unknown even to the Sanctuary. Dr. Asamori created three mechanical Steel Armors — Sky, Earth, and Sea — powered by microfusion batteries and kinetic energy conversion, requiring no cosmo to operate. The three pilots are **Sho of the Eagle** (Sky Climber), **Daichi of the Dolphin** (Land Liner), and **Ushio of the Wolf** (Marine Tunnel). Their combat method relies on "kinetic synchronization" — centrifugal force, air pressure, and physics against the metaphysical power of cosmo; their most notable feat was defeating Centaurus Babel by absorbing his sacred fire. The project was discontinued by Saori Kido due to the immense physical strain on the pilots, who lacked cosmo to protect their bodies. The technology was later revived and expanded in *Saint Seiya Omega* [9][10].

### 5. Scales (Poseidon's Mariners)

**Scales (鱗衣スケイル)** are the armor of Poseidon's **Mariners**, created during the "Time of the Gods" in anticipation of war between Athena and Poseidon over control of Attica. Their battlefield superiority actually forced Athena to create the Cloths to counteract them. Scales contain **Orichalcum** (the strongest metal on Earth, labeled "hypermetal"), giving them a golden-orange hue; stronger Scales appear to contain more of it. Unlike Cloths, Scales show no indication of being alive, have no ability to heal or evolve, and are stored in object form in Poseidon's Temple rather than Pandora boxes. Poseidon did not place a ban on weapons; the Chrysaor Scale comes equipped with a divine lance. Unlike Saints, who train for years, Mariners are **selected by the Scale** because they already possess the qualifications, granting them a base cosmo equivalent to the Seventh Sense [10][11]. Per the *Rise of Poseidon* manga, the Scales were forged by the **Cyclopes**, divine blacksmiths of Poseidon's kingdom, using pure orichalcum harvested from the heart of lost Atlantis; the versions seen in the classic saga were weakened "skeletons" of their primordial forms, and the true **Arcane Scales** awaken when Poseidon's blood is spilled on them [11].

### 6. Surplices (Hades' Specters)

**Surplices (冥衣サプリス)** are the infernal armor of Hades' **108 Specters**, the standing army of the Underworld. The number 108 is drawn from the classic Chinese novel *Water Margin* (36 heavenly and 72 earthly stars) and from Buddhist tradition, where 108 represents worldly desires; Kurumada inverted the symbolism — Hades' 108 serve death rather than liberation, corresponding to 108 malevolent stars (*Jashin-sei*). Surplices are forged from the essence of the Underworld, linked to the darkest constellations, and their power is generally comparable to Silver Cloths, with the elite **Three Judges of Hell** reaching Gold Cloth level. A notable trait: Surplices can be worn by souls, not just living bodies, allowing Specters to manifest even after death within the Underworld [12]. Fallen Gold Saints who are resurrected by Hades wear Surplice variants of their original Gold Cloths [4].

### 7. God Robes (Asgard's God Warriors)

The **God Robes (ゴッドローブ)** are the armor of Odin's **God Warriors**, legendary warriors of Asgard who appear exclusively in the **anime-only Asgard chapter** (episodes 74–99 of the 1986 anime), inserted between the Sanctuary and Poseidon arcs. Little is known of their origins, but they are believed to be ancient, sleeping hidden for thousands of years; notably, the legendary Siegfried did not wear a God Robe when he slew the dragon Fafnir during the Myth Age, suggesting the Robes were crafted later. Hilda, the High Priestess of Odin manipulated by Poseidon's Nibelungen Ring, awakened the Robes using the power of Polaris; the Robes themselves called forth their bearers. They exhibit great variety in design with coverage just below that of a Gold Cloth, withstood attacks from Saints possessing the Seventh Sense without major damage, and are linked to the stars of the Big Dipper. Each God Robe is adorned with an **Odin Sapphire**; all seven must be placed on Odin's statue to summon the **Odin Robe** and the legendary sword **Balmung** [13][14]. In the spin-off *Soul of Gold*, the God Warriors receive red Odin Sapphires that boost their cosmo while draining their souls [34].

### 8. God Cloths

The **God Cloths (神聖衣ゴッドクロス)** are a unique class of Cloths existing since the Age of Myth, transformed by spilling **Athena's blood** on the original Cloths when a Saint burns their cosmo "infinitely high." They provide protection and power that surpass Gold Saints and even minor gods like Thanatos and Hypnos, and are second only to the original armors of the 12 Olympians (the Kamui). Visually, they are exceedingly elaborate versions that almost completely cover the body, all equipped with wings. Key traits: near-absolute protection (Thanatos' *Terrible Providence*, which destroyed five Gold Cloths in one strike, could not scratch Seiya's Pegasus God Cloth); a massive cosmo boost enabling Saints to injure Olympian gods like Hades; and the ability to travel faster than light and fight at an atomic level. However, being made by mortal hands, they can be destroyed by gods — Hades pierced Seiya's armor. All five main Bronze Saints and all twelve Gold Saints have God Cloth forms [13]. In *The Lost Canvas*, the Pegasus Cloth can achieve God Cloth level at maximum cosmo without god's blood, after receiving Asmita's blood and Hakurei's repair [13].

### 9. Kamui (God Robes of Olympus)

**Kamui (神衣)** — also romanized *Shin'ei* — are the original divine garments of the 12 Olympian Gods, considered the most powerful type of armor in the mythos. Only one has been shown in battle: **Athena's Cloth**. Athena's Cloth sustained near-endless damage from Hades without visible damage, making it the most powerful Cloth in the series; only the full-power impact from Abzu (in *Omega*), the creator of the Universe, could deal extensive damage to it. Kamui have bulkier, more elaborate and ornate designs that almost completely cover the body, unlike lesser armors. When not in use for long periods, Athena's Cloth enters a hibernation-like state, becoming the Athena statue atop the Sanctuary, and can be restored by spilling Athena's blood onto it. Concept art shows Zeus also uses a Kamui; Poseidon and Hades have not been seen wearing their own Kamui, instead wearing a Scale and Surplice respectively [14].

### 10. Other Armor Classes

- **Corona Cloth** — from the film *The Legend of Crimson Youth*; worn by the three Corona Saints serving the Sun God Abel; exceeds Gold Saint strength and is protected by a corona flame of one million degrees Celsius [2].
- **Coma Berenices Cloth** — from the novel *Gigantomachia*; a jet-black Cloth sealed in Mount Etna, belonging to no standard rank, worn by a heretic Saint destined to seal the giant god Typhon [2].
- **Galaxies** — Mars' armors worn by Martian warriors in *Saint Seiya Omega* [2].
- **Cloth Stones / New Cloths / Omega Cloths** — in *Omega*, the Cloths emerge from jewels (Cloth Stones); later the Bronze Saints awaken "New Cloths" and ultimately Omega-level power [37].
- **Crown Armors** — four mysterious armors (Corona, Boneses, Lynx, Karina, and Phoenix) that appeared alongside the original 12 Gold, 24 Silver, and 48 Bronze armors; three accepted bearers when the Sun God Abel descended, but the Phoenix remained unclaimed until Ikki appeared [2].

### 1. The Bronze Saints

#### The Five Main Protagonists

##### Pegasus Seiya (天馬星座の星矢)

**Power level.** Seiya is the title protagonist, the Bronze Saint of Pegasus and reincarnation of the mythical Pegasus Tenma who first harmed the god Hades. Though ranked Bronze, he repeatedly defeats Gold Saints (Taurus Aldebaran, Gemini Saga), destroys Poseidon's Mainstay Pillar, defeats Thanatos the God of Death, and directly harms Hades. His power progression spans the Seventh Sense (light speed, Gold-level combat), the Eighth Sense in the Hades arc, and the Ninth Sense implied with his God Cloth [16][17]. He is proclaimed the future Gold Saint of Sagittarius and appears as the Sagittarius Gold Saint in *Omega* [16].

**Signature techniques.** 
- **Pegasus Ryūsei Ken (天馬流星拳 / Pegasus Meteor Fist)** — a barrage of ~100 punches per second, eventually matching Gold Saint light-speed strikes.
- **Pegasus Suisei Ken (天馬彗星拳 / Pegasus Comet Fist)** — a single powerful punch concentrating the meteor barrage.
- **Pegasus Rolling Crush** — a grappling/crushing throw.
- Inherits the **Sagittarius Gold Cloth** and its golden arrow [16][17].

**Key appearances.** Training under Eagle Marin in Greece; Galaxian Wars; Black Saints; Silver Saints arc; Twelve Houses of the Zodiac; Asgard (anime); Poseidon arc; Hades arc (Sanctuary, Inferno, Elysium); *Next Dimension*; *Omega*; *Episode G* spin-offs [16][17].

**Fate.** In the Hades arc, Seiya is stabbed in the heart by Hades and left comatose/cursed by Hades' sword (a three-day death curse in *Next Dimension*). Athena (Saori Kido) and Shun travel back in time to save him, triggering the *Next Dimension* storyline [16][17][36].

##### Dragon Shiryu (龍星座の紫龍)

**Power level.** A Sino-Japanese Saint trained under Libra Dohko at the Five Peaks of Rozan. Noted as the calmest, most collected of the five heroes; can unleash full potential without wearing a Cloth, learned Dohko's *Rozan Hyakuryūha* while blind after seeing it once, made Deathmask's Gold Cloth abandon its unworthy wearer, and defeated Marina General Krishna while blinded. His weakness is a literal weak point at his heart — the claw of the dragon tattoo. He is proclaimed future Gold Saint of Libra [17][18].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Rozan Shōryūha (廬山昇龍覇 / Rozan Rising Dragon Force)** — the dragon uppercut.
- **Rozan Ryūhishō (廬山龍飛翔 / Rozan Dragon Flight)**.
- **Rozan Hyakuryūha (廬山百龍覇 / Rozan Hundred Dragons Force)**.
- **Rozan Kōryūha (廬山亢龍覇 / Rozan Proud Dragon Lord)** — the forbidden kamikaze technique used against Shura.
- **Excalibur** — inherited from Capricorn Shura [17][18].

**Key appearances.** Galaxian Wars; Sanctuary arc (blinded himself fighting Perseus Algol; defeated Deathmask; fought Shura); Poseidon arc (blinded again, fought Krishna); Hades arc; *Next Dimension* (adopts a baby named Shoryu with Chunli) [17][18][40].

**Fate.** Survives the original series. In *Next Dimension* he and Chunli adopt a baby named Shoryu [40].

##### Cygnus Hyoga (白鳥星座の氷河)

**Power level.** Half-Russian, trained in Siberia under Aquarius Camus. Master of ice techniques who canonically surpassed his master by mastering **Absolute Zero** while weak and in pain. Can wear the Aquarius Gold Cloth; fought a Judge of the Underworld and survived. Proclaimed future Gold Saint of Aquarius. His weakness is his emotional nature [17][18].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Diamond Dust (ダイヤモンドダスト)** — ice crystals propelled by cold wind.
- **Aurora Thunder Attack (オーロラサンダーアタック)**.
- **Aurora Execution (オーロラエクスキューション)** — the supreme technique of Aquarius, inherited after surpassing Camus.
- **Freezing Coffin (フリージングコフィン)** [17][18].

**Key appearances.** Galaxian Wars; Sanctuary arc (battled his master Camus; killed Camus); Poseidon arc (lost vision in one eye saving Isaac in the backstory); Hades arc; *Omega* (defeats Tokisada); *Next Dimension* (proves himself to Mystoria of Aquarius) [17][18][34].

**Fate.** Survives the original series; appears in *Omega* as a legendary Saint; in *Next Dimension* he is officially acknowledged as the future heir of the Aquarius Gold Cloth [18][34].

##### Andromeda Shun (アンドロメダ星座の瞬)

**Power level.** A pacifist whose potential is widely considered the strongest of the five Bronze Saints. Evidence cited by fan analysts: he held his own against Saga, fought Aphrodite when he actively wanted to fight, awakened Seventh Sense potential before the Sanctuary battle, and used the devastating **Nebula Storm** without his Cloth. He was chosen as **Hades' vessel** because his cosmos was strong enough to host a god, and he even briefly gained the upper hand against Hades. He can wear the Virgo Cloth and is proclaimed future Gold Saint of Virgo [17][18][52].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Nebula Chain (ネビュラチェーン)** — chain-based offense/defense.
- **Thunder Wave (サンダーウェーブ)** — the Andromeda Chain's electric attack.
- **Nebula Stream (ネビュラストリーム)**.
- **Nebula Storm (ネビュラストーム)** — noted as one of the most powerful techniques in the franchise [17][18].

**Key appearances.** Galaxian Wars; Sanctuary arc (defeated Aphrodite); Poseidon arc; Hades arc (as Hades' host, freed himself from the god's possession); *Omega* (appears as a doctor); *Next Dimension* (time-travels to the 18th century with Athena); *The Lost Canvas* has his 18th-century predecessor [17][18][34][36].

**Fate.** Survives the original series; in *Omega* he is a doctor; in *Next Dimension* he travels back in time with Saori to the 18th century [34][36].

##### Phoenix Ikki (鳳凰星座の一輝)

**Power level.** Shun's older brother, trained on Death Queen Island under Guilty. Physically the strongest Bronze Saint and canonically above most Silver Saints; cannot truly die thanks to Phoenix regeneration, growing stronger each time he dies. He defeated two Silver Saints at once, fought Virgo Shaka (the "man closest to God") and scared him, and could hurt Thanatos while wearing only a Bronze Cloth (something Seiya couldn't do without a God Cloth). Proclaimed future Gold Saint of Leo [17][18][52].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Hō'ō Genma Ken (鳳凰幻魔拳 / Phoenix Illusion Demonic Fist)** — an illusion technique that makes victims psychologically believe they are being ripped to pieces.
- **Hō'ō Ten'yō Ken** — his powerful fire-based attack.
- **Phoenix's Wings Rise** [17][18].

**Key appearances.** Death Queen Island (training, dethroning Jango); Galaxian Wars (as antagonist leading the Black Saints); Sanctuary arc (fought Shaka); Poseidon arc; Hades arc (fought Aiacos and Minos); *Omega* (sacrifices himself to kill Aegaeon but survives) [17][18][34].

**Fate.** Survives; appears in *Omega* as a legendary Saint [18][34].

#### Supporting Bronze Saints

- **Unicorn Jabu (一角獣星座の邪武)** — trained in Algeria; defeated Lionet Ban in the Galaxian Wars before losing to Shun. Fought Specters at the start of the Hades arc in the manga (the anime skipped this scene) and later protected Seiya's sister Seika from Thanatos. Techniques: **Unicorn Gallop**, **Unicorn Head**. Survives; appears in *Omega* wearing a Steel Cloth [19][40].
- **Hydra Ichi (海ヘビ星座の市)** — uses poisonous claw attacks (**Mellow Poison**, **Splendid Fang**); appointed commander of Sanctuary guards alongside Nachi; protected Seika from Thanatos. In *Omega* he is promoted to Hydrus Silver Saint [18][34].
- **Bear Geki (大熊星座の檄)** — trained in the Canadian Rockies; his **Hanging Bear** neck hold exceeds 1,900 kg of pressure (up to 3,000 kg). Defeated by Seiya in the Galaxian Wars first round; protected Athena during the Twelve Temples battle; defeated by Mizar Syd in the anime-only Asgard arc; protected Seika in the Hades OVAs [20].
- **Lionet Ban (獅子星座の蛮)** — defeated by Jabu in the Galaxian Wars; participates in the same arcs as the other secondary Bronze Saints. Uses **Lion's Bite** [18][40].
- **Wolf Nachi (狼星座の那智)** — trained in Liberia; defeated by Ikki's Genma Ken in the Galaxian Wars; appointed co-commander of Sanctuary guards with Ichi; risked his life protecting Seika from Thanatos. Technique: **Dead Howling**. Survives; his successor in *Omega* is Wolf Yoshitomi [21].
- **Chameleon June (カメレオン星座のジュネ)** — a female Bronze Saint trained on Andromeda Island, Shun's fellow student and love interest [18][40].

### 2. The Silver Saints

##### Eagle Marin (鷲星座の魔鈴)

**Power level.** A female Silver Saint of the Aquila (Eagle) constellation, teacher of Pegasus Seiya. As a Silver Saint she possesses advanced cosmo mastery, superhuman strength, and Mach 2–5 movement. She and Ophiuchus Shaina were the first Silver Saints introduced by Kurumada. In the manga she is notably powerful — she defeats Hound Asterion and escapes the Sanctuary — though the anime depicts her as weaker (an "Adaptational Wimp," losing battles despite being powerful in the manga) [22][26].

**Signature techniques.** **Meteor Punches (流星拳)** — the technique she taught Seiya; **Eagle Toe Flash** — a diving kick used to defeat Asterion; **Empty Fist (空拳)** — a deceptive maneuver [22].

**Key appearances.** Trained Seiya in Greece; Silver Saints arc (secretly helped Seiya with Aries Mu's aid; exposed by Asterion's mind-reading but escaped and defeated him); discovered the real Pope Shion's corpse at Star Hill; Hades arc (protected Seika from Thanatos); anime-only Asgard arc (beaten by Megrez Delta Alberich). She is searching for her missing younger brother Toma, whose existence remains canon even though the *Heaven Chapter Overture* movie was removed from canon [22].

**Fate.** Marin and Shaina are the only known Silver Saints alive at the end of the original series [22].

##### Ophiuchus Shaina (蛇遣い星座のシャイナ)

**Power level.** Silver Saint of Ophiuchus, considered one of the strongest Saints who never awakened the Seventh Sense; a "Lightning Bruiser" who blocked Seiya's 85 punches per second while counting them. Per the Law of the Masks, a female Saint whose face is seen by a man must either kill him or fall in love with him — Seiya broke her mask, triggering her unrequited love for him [23][26].

**Signature techniques.** **Thunder Claw** — her only known technique [23][26].

**Key appearances.** One of the most recurring characters in the franchise: pursues Seiya in the manga; brutally beats Marin in an anime "training match"; protects Seiya from Leo Aiolia and confesses her love; appears in *Saintia Shō*, the Asgard arc, the Poseidon arc, the Hades arc, *Next Dimension*, and *Omega* (where she becomes Pegasus Kōga's master) [23].

**Fate.** Survives the original series; becomes Kōga's master in *Omega* [23][34].

##### Lyra Orphée (琴座のオルフェ)

**Power level.** The "Legendary Saint" — despite being a Silver Saint, his abilities surpass those of the Gold Saints. It is hinted he awakened the Eighth Sense, as he entered the Underworld alive. He is ranked #1 in fan power rankings of Silver Saints [5][24].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Stringer Nocturne** — a melody generating a powerful energy burst that pulverizes body and armor.
- **Stringer Fine (Final Chord)** — his most powerful technique; strings wrap the enemy and tighten, causing a cosmo explosion.
- **Death Trip Serenade** — a spell-like melody causing all who hear it to fall into a ten-day sleep; successfully put Pandora, Minos, and Aiacos to sleep [24].

**Key appearances.** Hades arc only: he descended into the Underworld to plead with Hades for his beloved Eurydice's life; deceived by Pandora's mirror, he stayed in the Underworld playing his lyre. He initially incapacitates Seiya and Shun, then turns against the Specter Sphinx Pharaoh when he learns the truth, defeats Pharaoh, and confronts Hades directly at Giudecca [24].

**Fate.** Dies in Seiya's arms after being critically wounded by Rhadamanthys, sacrificing his body to give Seiya an opening [24].

##### Lizard Misty (蜥蜴星座のミスティ)

**Power level.** A narcissistic, beautiful Silver Saint known as "The Silver Saint," who was never once harmed in battle before his final mission. In the manga he blows up Mount Fuji; he believes only true victory is winning without getting hurt [26].

**Signature techniques.** **Mavrou Trippa** (Black Hole) and **Air Shield** [26].

**Key appearances.** Silver Saints arc; dispatched to kill the Bronze Saints. Defeated by Seiya (with Marin's secret aid) [22][26].

**Fate.** Dies peacefully with a smile after acknowledging Seiya's merits [26].

##### Perseus Algol (ペルセウス星座のアルゴル)

**Power level.** Leader of the second group of Silver Saints sent to kill the Bronze Saints, named after the "Demon Star" Algol. His **Medusa Shield** petrifies those who look at it [25].

**Signature techniques.** **Ra's Al Ghûl Gorgonio** — a leaping kick with an illusion of serpents; the **Medusa Shield** [25].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc: petrified Shun, Seiya, and Hyoga; Shiryu fought him blindfolded, then blinded himself and defeated Algol with Rozan Shōryūha. In the anime, the Steel Saints aided Shiryu, and Sky Sho absorbed the Medusa Shield's energy. He was later resurrected by Hades with a Surplice, along with Auriga Capella and Cerberus Dante, and all three attacked Shiryu at Rozan but were defeated by his Rozan Shōryūha [25].

**Fate.** Killed by Shiryu; resurrected by Hades in a Surplice; killed again by Shiryu [25].

##### Other Notable Silver Saints

- **Hound Asterion** — part of the first group of Silver Saints; can read opponents' minds with **Satori no Hō** and uses **Million Ghost Attack**. Defeated by Marin when she emptied her thoughts [22][26].
- **Whale Moses** — physically strong; uses **Kaitos Spouting Bomber**; introduced himself by splitting the ocean in two. Defeated by Seiya [26].
- **Centaurus Babel** — uses fire attacks ("sacred fire"); gained the upper hand over Hyoga in the anime until the Steel Saints interfered; defeated by Hyoga [26].
- **Crow Jamian** — commands crows; sent to kidnap Saori Kido [26].
- **Cerberus Dante** — uses steel spike balls and chains against Ikki; resurrected by Hades in a Surplice and defeated by Shiryu at Rozan [25][26].
- **Auriga Capella** — attacks with flying saucers; resurrected by Hades and defeated by Shiryu [25][26].
- **Canis Major Sirius, Heracles Algethi, Musca Dio** — three Silver Saints sent after Seiya in the hospital; all defeated when Seiya donned the Sagittarius Gold Cloth [26].
- **Sagitta Ptolemy** — the last Silver Saint sent; killed by Seiya in a single attack [26].
- **Cepheus Daidalos (Albiore in the anime)** — Shun and June's master at Andromeda Island; died in battle against Aphrodite [26].

### 3. The Gold Saints

#### Collective Profile

The Gold Saints are "the twelve most powerful and highest-ranking warriors in Athena's army," serving as the last line of defense of the Sanctuary and Athena herself, from among whom the Grand Pope is generally selected [4]. Their power derives from mastery of the Seventh Sense, granting light-speed movement; some have reached the Eighth Sense. A battle between two equally matched Gold Saints leads to a "Thousand Day War" stalemate. Three Gold Saints can unleash the forbidden **Athena Exclamation**, and all twelve can perform the "Sacrifice of the Gold Saints" [4]. All twelve Gold Saints earned their Cloths during childhood (at ages 7–15) and come from nations around the world [4][26].

**Official power ranking context.** Sources differ on the "strongest" Gold Saint. Kurumada's databook (the *Taizen*) and the author himself state that **Shion at peak > Saga**, while *Episode G* says **Aiolos is the strongest** of his generation (Mu, Shaka, Shura, and the narrator all state this); Saga himself calls **Dohko the strongest** [28]. Fan consensus generally places Saga as the strongest of the original 12 at the time of the Sanctuary arc, with Shaka as "the man closest to God" [4][26][28].

#### Mu of Aries (牡羊座のムウ)

**Power level.** Age 20, Tibetan, trained at Jamir. Possesses the strongest psychic powers of his generation (psychokinesis, teleportation, telepathy) and is the only master of Cloth repair, a descendant of the alchemists of the lost continent of Mu. In the Hades OVA his character was somewhat weakened compared to the manga [4][26].

**Signature techniques.** **Crystal Wall (クリスタルウォール)** — an "indestructible" psychic barrier; **Crystal Net**; **Stardust Revolution (スターダストレボリューション)** — an attack summoning stellar fragments at light speed, comparable to Dohko's Rozan Hyakuryūha; **Starlight Extinction (スターライトエクスティンクション)** — a secret killing technique he created at age seven [4][26][31].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (helped the Bronze Saints directly, repairing their Cloths); Hades arc (fought the resurrected Shion and the traitor Gold Saints; participated in the Athena Exclamation clash against Saga, Camus, and Shura); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard; performed the Athena Exclamation with Saga and Dohko) [4][26][34].

**Fate.** Dies with all twelve Gold Saints when they sacrifice their lives to destroy the Wailing Wall in the Hades arc; revived in Asgard in *Soul of Gold* [4][34].

#### Aldebaran of Taurus (牡牛座のアルデバラン)

**Power level.** Age 20, Brazilian, 210 cm/130 kg. Physically the strongest Gold Saint, described as a "wall incarnate"; despite his bulk, he is noted as the fastest of the Gold Saints [4][26].

**Signature technique.** **Great Horn (グレートホーン)** — an iaijutsu-style quick-draw launching waves of cosmo to pulverize enemies [4][26].

**Key appearances.** Twelve Temples arc (honored a promise to let Seiya pass after Seiya broke his horn); Hades arc (killed by Specters at the Taurus Temple — sources differ on whether it was Papillon Myu or Deep Niobe — but delivered a time-delayed killing blow on his attacker); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard) [4][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Hades arc, his horn cut; later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26][34].

#### Saga of Gemini (双子座のサガ)

**Power level.** Age 28, Greek. Considered the strongest Gold Saint of his generation and the most powerful Saint to have served Athena in the original series; described as a "force of nature." Kurumada states his **Galaxian Explosion** has the power to pulverize stars. He murdered Grand Pope Shion, took the Sanctuary throne for 13 years, and almost killed baby Athena. Fan analysis differentiates "White Saga" and "Black Saga" (his evil-transformed state), concluding Black Saga's combat power surpasses White Saga's [4][26][31].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Galaxian Explosion (ギャラクシアンエクスプロージョン)** — his ultimate attack, resembling planets crashing; "never survived" by any foe.
- **Another Dimension (アナザーディメンション)** — tears holes in spacetime, banishing enemies.
- **Genrō Maōken (幻朧魔皇拳 / Demon Emperor Fist)** — a forbidden brainwashing technique, permitted only for the Pope; Saga's version is noted as infinitely close to Shion's [4][26][31].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (the Big Bad; fought all five Bronze Saints at once; defeated but stopped only when he sacrificed himself in redemption); Hades arc (resurrected by Hades in a Surplice; with Camus and Shura killed Shaka via the Athena Exclamation — secretly acting for Athena's sake; participated in the Athena Exclamation clash against Mu, Aiolia, and Milo); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard) [4][25][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Sanctuary arc (one of five Gold Saints killed by the Bronze Saints during the Twelve Houses battle); resurrected in the Hades arc; sacrifices himself again — casting Galaxian Explosion to let Seiya through — and ultimately dies with all twelve Gold Saints at the Wailing Wall [4][26][33].

#### Kanon of Gemini (双子座のカノン)

**Power level.** Saga's twin brother, who "became stronger than Saga by the Hades Chapter after Saga's death." He has identical moves to Saga (Another Dimension, Galaxian Explosion) plus his own **Golden Triangle** variant, and the Demon Emperor Fist [4][26][32].

**Signature techniques.** **Galaxian Explosion**; **Another Dimension**; **Golden Triangle** — his dimension-bending technique; **Genrō Maōken** [26][32].

**Key appearances.** Poseidon arc (the antagonist manipulating Poseidon's Mariners before repenting); Hades arc (redeemed, served as the Gemini Gold Saint; mentally controlled and killed the Specter Balrog Lune; challenged Wyvern Rhadamanthys, defeating him with ease when his power was not restricted) [26][32][40].

**Fate.** Died in the Hades arc fighting Rhadamanthys; killed the Judge with Galaxian Explosion and died [26][32].

#### Deathmask of Cancer (蟹座のデスマスク)

**Power level.** Age 23, Italian. The cruelest Gold Saint, one of the "evil" Gold Saints who believed "only strength could bring justice." His Sekishiki techniques send souls to Yomotsu Hirasaka (the Cancer entrance to the Underworld) [4][21][26].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Sekishiki Meikaiha (積尸気冥界波 / Dark World Wave)** — separates body and soul, sending the soul to the Underworld; ineffective against Specters.
- **Sekishiki Konsoha (積尸気魂葬波 / Soul Burial Wave)** — makes nearby spirits' souls explode.
- **Sekishiki Metsu Meikaiha** — tears open a rift creating a vortex that devours souls, matter, and energy [21][26].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (fought Shiryu; threw Shunrei off a cliff from miles away; his Cancer Cloth abandoned him when faced with a Saint who embodied love and justice; Gigantomachia shows he still had good in him); Hades arc (resurrected as a Surplice-wearing Gold Saint; genuinely behaved like a Saint of Athena, submitting to humiliating roles for humanity's sake); Soul of Gold (heroic personality makeover, secretly caring for a flower girl named Helena) [21][23][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Twelve Temples arc by Shiryu (pushed into Yomotsu Hirasaka); killed again in the Hades arc as a resurrected Surplice-wearing Gold Saint; later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][21][26].

#### Aiolia of Leo (獅子座のアイオリア)

**Power level.** Age 20, Greek. A straightforward, righteous Saint; the "reincarnation of Achilles" in *Episode G*, with an electric cosmo that could kill gods. He is the brother of Aiolos, falsely labeled a traitor's brother, and the main protagonist of *Soul of Gold* [4][26][34].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Lightning Bolt (ライトニングボルト)** — destructive energy capsule; his core technique.
- **Lightning Plasma (ライトニングプラズマ)** — 100 million punches per second at its strongest [4][26].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (one of the Saori Kido believers; fought Seiya at the Leo Temple; brainwashed by Saga in the anime, killing Cassios); Hades arc (fought the resurrected traitor Gold Saints; participated in the Athena Exclamation clash; defeated by Rhadamanthys at Hades Castle where power was reduced to 1/10); Soul of Gold (the protagonist; faced Loki at the climax with Odin's sword and Draupnir) [4][26][34].

**Fate.** Dies with all twelve Gold Saints at the Wailing Wall; later revived in Asgard in *Soul of Gold* [4][34].

#### Shaka of Virgo (乙女座のシャカ)

**Power level.** Age 20, Indian. Called "the man closest to God"; considered the reincarnation of Buddha with the most potent cosmo among all Saints. By simply opening his eyes he creates a shockwave comparable to the energy of the Big Bang. He is the only Gold Saint with full control of the Eighth Sense. He defeated Seiya, Shiryu, and Shun simultaneously and fought Phoenix Ikki to a draw, removing his senses one by one [4][26][28].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Tenbu Hōrin (天舞宝輪 / Treasure of Heaven)** — his signature attack/defense: creates a universe where opponents cannot attack or escape while having their five senses (and the sixth) removed one by one.
- **Rikudō Rinne (六道輪廻)** — sends souls to one of the six realms of existence.
- **Khan (カーン)** — a barrier that reflects attacks back with malice.
- **Tenma Kōfuku** — a massive direct attack comparable to the Galaxian Explosion.
- **Agyō (阿形)** — opening his eyes alone creates a shockwave comparable to the Big Bang [4][26][28].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (fought Ikki; a fight with Aiolia was a draw stopped by the Pope); Hades arc (faced resurrected Saga, Shura, and Camus alone; consumed by the Athena Exclamation but survived using the Eighth Sense, having staged his death to infiltrate the Underworld; confronted Hades directly and helped reunite the Twelve Gold Saints); *Next Dimension* (his spirit fights Virgo Shijima across time and reveals Shun will be his successor); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard; fought God Warrior Balder) [4][26][28][34].

**Fate.** Killed (seemingly) by the Athena Exclamation from Saga, Camus, and Shura, but survived via the Eighth Sense; his final death comes with the Wailing Wall sacrifice of all twelve Gold Saints [4][26][28][33].

#### Dohko of Libra (天秤座の童虎)

**Power level.** Age 261 (a young man in the 1747 Holy War), Chinese. One of the two survivors of the previous Holy War (with Shion); mentor of Dragon Shiryu, known as "Old Master" (Rōshi). Saga himself calls Dohko the strongest Gold Saint. After the previous war, Dohko was assigned to guard Hades' seal and received the **Misopetha-Menos** ability, slowing his heartbeat to ~100,000 beats per year so he aged at 1/365th the normal rate. He mastered both the Seventh and Eighth Senses [4][26][29].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Rozan Shōryūha (廬山昇龍覇)** — upward blow manifesting a great dragon.
- **Rozan Hyakuryūha (廬山百龍覇)** — releasing hundreds of dragons; his signature technique.
- **Misopetha-Menos** — the aging-slowing ability [4][26][29].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (sent his Gold Cloth to free Hyoga from the Freezing Coffin; saved Shunrei from Deathmask); Asgard/Poseidon arcs (ordered the Gold Saints to remain in Sanctuary; had Kiki deliver the Libra Gold Cloth to the Bronze Saints); Hades arc (fought the resurrected Shion, breaking Misopetha-Menos to regain his youthful form; told the Bronze Saints about the Eighth Sense; broke the Wailing Wall with the combined power of all 12 Gold Saints); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard; performed the Athena Exclamation with Mu and Saga) [4][26][29][34].

**Fate.** Dies at the Wailing Wall sacrifice in the Hades arc (not of old age); later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26][29][34].

#### Milo of Scorpio (蠍座のミロ)

**Power level.** Age 20, Greek. Praised as "one of the stronger men among the Gold Saints" whose speed is "godly"; nicknamed "The Swift Saint" and "The Crimson Gold Saint." He is Camus' close friend. In the CG movie *Legend of Sanctuary*, Milo's gender was changed to female [26][30].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Scarlet Needle (スカーレットニードル)** — 14 strikes creating a Scorpius constellation pattern on the opponent's body; each needle causes bleeding, pain, and loss of the senses; the final strike is **Antares (アンタレス)**, guaranteeing death.
- **Restriction (レストリクション)** — stops opponents' movements by attacking the central nervous system.
- **Scarlet Cyclone / Crimson Stinger** — enhanced variations [4][26][30].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (defeated Seiya, Shiryu, and Hyoga; allowed Hyoga to pass after Hyoga froze his star points; later used his own blood to repair the Cygnus Cloth); Hades arc (tested Kanon's redemption with 14 Scarlet Needles; fought Saga, Camus, and Shura; defeated by Rhadamanthys at Hades Castle); Soul of Gold (sacrificed himself to destroy a Great Root; reconciled with Camus) [26][30][34].

**Fate.** Dies with all twelve Gold Saints at the Wailing Wall; later revived in Asgard in *Soul of Gold* [26][30][34].

#### Aiolos of Sagittarius (射手座のアイオロス)

**Power level.** Died at age 14, 13 years before the Sanctuary battles; Greek. Shion's favorite to become Grand Master, surpassing even Saga in integrity and potential. The official encyclopedia states his fighting ability equals Saga's, and the *Meikai Hen* Gold Saint Files define him as even stronger; *Episode G* states he is the strongest Gold Saint of his generation several times. His spirit resides within the Sagittarius armor, intervening at critical moments [4][26][27].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Atomic Thunderbolt (アトミックサンダーボルト)** — anime-only technique concentrating cosmo into electrical energy balls fired at light speed.
- **Golden Arrow** — the Sagittarius Cloth's bow and arrow; delivers the final blow in four of the five original films when used by Seiya.
- **Infinity Break** — listed among his techniques [4][26][27].

**Key appearances.** Backstory: selected by Pope Shion as his successor; jealous Saga killed Shion, disguised himself as Pope, and attempted to kill infant Athena; Aiolos rescued Athena but was branded a traitor, wounded by Capricorn Shura (refusing to fight at full strength), and entrusted Athena and the Sagittarius Gold Cloth to Mitsumasa Kido before dying. After death, his spirit repeatedly aided Seiya, left a hidden message in the Temple of Sagittarius, and at the Wailing Wall guided all twelve Saints' attacks into one focused strike. He is revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26][27].

**Fate.** Died of his injuries before the main story began; his spirit returns at the Wailing Wall; revived in Asgard in *Soul of Gold* [4][26][27][34].

#### Shura of Capricorn (山羊座のシュラ)

**Power level.** Age 23, Spanish. Possesses the **Sacred Sword Excalibur** in his arms and legs, capable of cutting anything; some Capricorn Saints (Izo, El Cid) had even stronger versions. He killed Aiolos on the orders of the false Pope, believing him a traitor [4][26].

**Signature techniques.** **Excalibur (エクスカリバー)** — the holy sword channeled into arms and legs, with variations like Double Excalibur; **Jumping Stone** [4][26].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (fought Shiryu; Shiryu used the forbidden Rozan Kōryūha; Shura redeemed himself, realizing everything he believed was a lie, and gave up his Gold Cloth to save Shiryu); Hades arc (resurrected by Hades in a Surplice; with Saga and Camus killed Shaka via the Athena Exclamation); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard) [4][25][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Twelve Temples arc by Shiryu; killed again in the Hades arc as a resurrected Surplice-wearing Gold Saint; later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26].

#### Camus of Aquarius (水瓶座のカミュ)

**Power level.** Age 20, French. Master of ice control, called the "Magician of ice and water"; uniquely slows down atoms rather than moving them. He is Hyoga's true master (Kurumada originally intended Milo but changed his mind) [4][26][30].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Aurora Execution (オーロラエクスキューション)** — unleashes fighting spirit approaching Absolute Zero.
- **Freezing Coffin (フリージングコフィン)** — an "indestructible" Absolute Zero ice block.
- **Diamond Dust (ダイヤモンドダスト)** — ice crystals propelled by cold wind [4][26].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (froze Hyoga in the Freezing Coffin; fought and was killed by his student Hyoga in an emotional confrontation); Hades arc (resurrected by Hades in a Surplice; with Saga and Shura killed Shaka via the Athena Exclamation); Soul of Gold (revived in Asgard; childhood friend of God Warrior Surtr) [4][25][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Twelve Temples arc by Hyoga; killed again in the Hades arc as a resurrected Surplice-wearing Gold Saint; later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26].

#### Aphrodite of Pisces (魚座のアフロディーテ)

**Power level.** Age 22, Swedish. Believes "strength is justice" but wishes more than anyone for peace on earth. Fights with roses and can communicate with and control plants [4][26].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Royal Demon Rose (ロイヤルデモンローズ)** — poisonous red rose projectiles.
- **Piranha Rose (ピラニアンローズ)** — black roses that destroy anything, including armor.
- **Bloody Rose (ブラッディローズ)** — an undodgeable white rose that drains all blood from the heart [4][26].

**Key appearances.** Sanctuary arc (ambushed Silver Saint Albiore with the Poison Rose in the anime; guardian of the last temple, defeated by Shun); Hades arc (resurrected as a Surplice-wearing Gold Saint); Soul of Gold (heroic personality makeover; survived inside Yggdrasil due to poison immunity, becoming key to everyone's survival) [4][26][34].

**Fate.** Killed in the Twelve Temples arc by Shun; killed again in the Hades arc; later revived in *Soul of Gold* [4][26].

#### Shion of Aries (牡羊座のシオン) — Former Pope

**Power level.** Shion was the Aries Gold Saint of the previous Holy War (18th century), teacher of Mu, and Grand Pope murdered by Saga. Both the *Taizen* and Kurumada state that **Shion at peak > Saga**. He mastered psychokinesis, teleportation, the Light Speed Punch, and the forbidden **Genrō Maōken** (noting Saga's demon fist is infinitely close to Shion's). He can read the memories of a Cloth's previous owner through High Synchronicity [4][26][31].

**Signature techniques.**
- **Stardust Revolution (スターダストレボリューション)** — his ultimate technique, summoning stellar fragments at light speed; comparable to Dohko's Rozan Hyakuryūha.
- **Starlight Extinction** — the secret killing technique created by Mu (Shion learned it by reading the Aries Gold Cloth's memories).
- **Kōsen (cloth repair)** — the alchemist repair technique; Cloths evolve and become stronger each time they are repaired.
- **Golden Light** — his ultimate technique in the final battle against Hades: summons all Gold Cloths and releases their accumulated solar energy, burning the fully resurrected Hades and expelling his soul from Alone's body [31].

**Key appearances.** Previous Holy War (survived alongside Dohko); original series (the murdered Pope whose corpse Marin discovers at Star Hill, revealing Saga's deception); Hades arc (resurrected by Hades in a Surplice; fought Dohko, breaking his Misopetha-Menos; ultimately came to Athena's side) [4][25][31].

**Fate.** Murdered by Saga before the series; resurrected by Hades in the Hades arc; sacrifices himself with the twelve Gold Saints at the Wailing Wall [4][31][33].

### 4. The Marina Generals of Poseidon

The **Mariners** are the servants of Poseidon, god of the seas. Despite being only seven generals, they are as strong as — and possibly stronger than — Gold Saints. Each represents one of the Earth's oceans and a mythological sea monster. They are selected by their Scales, which grant them a base cosmo equivalent to the Seventh Sense without training [11][38]. The elite officers are called Haejanggun (General), equivalent to Gold Saints, with golden armor; regular Mariners are roughly Bronze/Silver Saint level [11].

- **Kanon of Sea Dragon** — See Saga's twin brother above. As a Marina General, he manipulated the entire underwater army and mastered the "Golden Triangle" (banishing opponents to space-time rifts) and "Imperial Satan" mind control. He freed Poseidon's soul after being imprisoned by Saga. Later made a heel-face turn and became the Gemini Gold Saint in the Hades arc [38][40].
- **Krishna of Chrysaor** — Guardian of the Indian Ocean pillar, born in Sri Lanka. Wields Poseidon's divine golden spear, capable of piercing anything (it pierced Shiryu's shield). His ultimate technique, **Maharoini** (Great Illumination), uses kundalini energy from the seven chakras to create an impenetrable spiritual barrier, directly comparable to Virgo Shaka's defense. Defeated by Shiryu, who destroyed his pillar with Capricorn's Excalibur sword [11][38].
- **Sorento of Siren** — Guardian of the South Atlantic pillar, Austrian. His flute technique **Final Symphony of Death** attacks the central nervous system and cosmo directly, reducing an opponent's fighting strength to just 1%; he made Aldebaran of Taurus vulnerable. Described as the perfect counter for brute-force warriors. **He is the only Mariner to survive the war**, traveled with Julian Solo, and helped him make amends [11][38].
- **Isaac of Kraken** — Guardian of the Arctic Pacific pillar, Finnish. Trained in Siberia with Hyoga (under the Crystal Saint in the anime, or Camus in the manga); lost his left eye to an iceberg while saving Hyoga. His **Aurora Borealis** technique carries freezing power equivalent to the Gold Saints of Aquarius. Fought Hyoga at the Arctic Pacific pillar and severely injured him despite Hyoga possessing the Seventh Sense. Died in Hyoga's arms, revealing suspicions about Kanon [11][38].
- **Mermaid Thetis** — The only female Mariner, Poseidon's left hand. Saved as a fish by a young Julian Solo, she became loyal to him and died saving him from drowning in the manga [38].
- **Sea Horse Baian** — Uses air/wind attacks: **God Breath**, **Rising Billows** [38].
- **Scylla Io** — A beastmaster who summons six creatures (wolf, bear, bat, giant bee, eagle, snake) [38].
- **Lyumnades Caça** — The most vicious Mariner; a master of illusion and shapeshifter who uses telepathy to impersonate loved ones [38].

**Poseidon (Julian Solo).** The god of the seas, rival of Athena. In the Poseidon arc he is awakened by Kanon, who places the Nibelungen Ring on him, and attacks the world from his underwater temple. He is defeated by the Bronze Saints, and in the manga his soul is resealed by Athena. He does not wear his Kamui, instead wearing a Scale in his mortal vessel [1][38][40].

### 5. The God Warriors of Asgard

The **God Warriors** are the legendary warriors of Odin who protect Asgard, appearing exclusively in the anime-only Asgard chapter of the 1986 series (and in the spin-off *Soul of Gold*). They wear **God Robes** linked to the stars of the Big Dipper and carry **Odin Sapphires** [13][14]. All seven God Robes appear equal in strength, with no rank hierarchy, and they withstood attacks from Seventh-Sense Saints without major damage, though they may still be inferior to Gold Cloths [13].

- **Dubhe Alpha: Siegfried** — The strongest God Warrior; a descendant of the legendary Siegfried who slew the dragon Fafnir. His techniques include **Dragon Bravest Blizzard** and the **Odin Sword**. He voluntarily removed his Odin Sapphire and gave it to Seiya before facing Sorrento. He was defeated by Seiya [14][40].
- **Merak Beta: Hagen** — Master of both ice and fire, using techniques like **Great Ardent Blizzard** and **Demon Dragon Blood**. Loyal to Hilda [14][40].
- **Phecda Gamma: Thor** — Over 8 feet tall; a giant warrior wielding the hammer **Mjolnir** with the technique **Titanic Hercules**. Defeated by Shiryu [14][40].
- **Megrez Delta: Alberich** — A cunning strategist using the **Amethyst Sealed** technique (trapping opponents in amethyst crystals) and **Nature Unity** (absorbing nature's power). His gauntlets contain acid. Defeated by Hyoga [14][40].
- **Alioth Epsilon: Fenrir** — Raised by wolves after his parents' death; uses **Wolf Cruelty Claw**. Defeated by Shun [14][40].
- **Mizar Zeta: Syd** — The first God Warrior introduced; defeated Taurus Aldebaran (who was weakened by the Nibelungen Ring's curse). Uses **Viking Tiger Claw** and **Blue Impulse**. Defeated by Hyoga [14][40].
- **Alcor Zeta: Bud** — Syd's shadow, a twin who lived in obscurity; wears a white replica of his brother's armor (not a true God Robe, since Alcor is not part of the Big Dipper). Uses **Shadow Viking Tiger Claw** [13][14].
- **Benetnasch Eta: Mime** — A lyre-playing musician and the adopted son of the God Warrior who killed his parents; uses **Stringer Requiem** (sound-based attacks) and **Illusion Whole**. In the end he reveals his true nature as the son of the defeated warrior [14][40].

**Odin and the Odin Robe.** Odin is the king of Asgard, the Nordic god of wisdom and warfare, who wears the **Odin Robe** and wields the sword **Balmung**, which can break the curse Poseidon applied to Hilda. In times of need, Odin allowed **Seiya to wear his Robe** to save Asgard. At the end of the Asgard arc, no God Warriors were left alive, but Asgard remains protected as long as the Odin Robe remains there [13][14].

### 6. The Specters of Hades

The **Specters (冥闘士スペクター)** are the 108 warriors of Hades, chosen from the most powerful and corrupt souls in human history, resurrected at each holy war. They wear **Surplices**, draw power from their acceptance of death, and are designated by "Heavenly ___ Star" or "Earthly ___ Star" titles corresponding to the 108 malevolent stars [12][39].

#### The Three Judges of Hell (三巨頭)

- **Wyvern Rhadamanthys (天猛星ワイバーンのラダマンティス)** — The first Judge shown; Pandora's right-hand man, a brutal but capable leader, described as the most capable and intelligent element of Hades' army. He fought three Gold Saints simultaneously at Hades Castle (where his barrier limited their power to 1/10) and defeated them; he resisted Orphée's Death Trip Serenade. His signature move is **Greatest Caution** (a wind-type attack; **Giant Swing** crushes the soul). He was ultimately killed by **Gemini Kanon** with the Galaxian Explosion [30][32][39].
- **Griffon Minos (天貴星グリフォンのミーノス)** — The second Judge introduced; has versatile techniques and is the only Judge with actual duties in Hell — judging the recently deceased. His attack **Cosmic Marionette** uses invisible power cords to manipulate opponents like puppets. He died by explosion at the entrance of Elysium [12][39].
- **Garuda Aiacos (天雄星ガルーダのアイアコス)** — The last Judge introduced; cocky and overconfident, described as the swiftest and sharpest. His attack **Garuda Flap** uses wind from his wings. He was put to sleep by Orphée's Death Trip Serenade during the assault on Giudecca [12][39].

#### Other Notable Specters

- **Papillon Myu** — An ambiguously human Specter who pupates from a worm-monster to a butterfly-boy form; in some accounts he kills Aldebaran [26][39].
- **Sphinx Pharaoh** — An evil counterpart to Lyra Orphée; uses music to rip hearts out. Defeated by Orphée [24][39].
- **Acheron Charon** — The ferryman of the Underworld based on Greek myth; uses a paddle as a weapon and is "affably evil" [39].
- **Pandora Heinstein** — Hades' right hand who commands his armies; originally a human child who unwittingly released the seal containing Hypnos and Thanatos. She wields a trident and plays the harp. She is killed by Thanatos after giving Ikki the rosary that allowed him to travel to Elysium [39].
- **Deep Niobe** — The Specter who (in some accounts) killed Aldebaran with his fragrance-based attack, only to die himself from Aldebaran's time-delayed Great Horn [26].
- **Balrog Lune** — Killed by Kanon via mental control at Hades Castle [32].
- **Hypnos and Thanatos** — The twin gods of Sleep and Death, the true power behind Hades' army. Thanatos destroys five Gold Cloths in one strike (*Terrible Providence*) but is defeated by God Cloth Seiya; Hypnos is defeated by God Cloth Ikki and Shun [13][39].

**The Fallen Gold Saints.** In the Hades arc, Hades resurrects the deceased Gold Saints — Gemini Saga, Aquarius Camus, Capricorn Shura, Cancer Deathmask, Pisces Aphrodite, and Aries Shion — as Surplice-wearing Specters. They are given 12 hours to kill Athena or return to the Land of the Dead; most secretly act for Athena's sake [4][25].

## Part III: Major Spin-offs and Alternate Continuities

### Saint Seiya: Next Dimension (2006–2024)

*Next Dimension* is written and illustrated by Masami Kurumada himself and is **confirmed as canonical** to his original manga. It is both a direct sequel (Seiya survives Hades' curse but is dying) and a prequel (Athena and Shun travel back in time to the 18th-century Holy War). The 18th-century plot follows **Pegasus Tenma** (Seiya's past incarnation), his best friend **Alone** (Hades' vessel), and the young Gold Saints Aries Shion and Libra Dohko. The major antagonist is **Ophiuchus Odysseus**, the cursed 13th Gold Saint — the reincarnation of Asclepius, regarded as "without a doubt the most powerful gold knight in the entire history of the franchise," whose temple appears between Scorpio and Sagittarius. The new 1747 Gold Saints include Taurus Ox, Gemini twins Abel & Cain (Cain is admitted by Odysseus himself to be the strongest in direct combat), Cancer Deathtoll, Leo Kaiser (with his armored lion Goldie), Virgo Shijima, Scorpio Écarlate, Sagittarius Gestalt, Capricorn Izo, Aquarius Mystoria, and Pisces Cardinale. In the end, Apollo wipes out Athena and the Saints' memories as punishment for their time-travel [1][36].

### Saint Seiya: The Lost Canvas (2006–2011)

*The Lost Canvas* is Shiori Teshirogi's alternate-universe prequel set during the 18th-century Holy War, generally considered **non-canon** due to contradictions with *Next Dimension*. It humanizes the Specters and features a complete roster of 18th-century Gold Saints, most of whom die heroic deaths: **Albafica of Pisces** (poisoned blood sacrifice against Minos), **Hasgard of Taurus** (died standing after defeating two Specters), **Asmita of Virgo** (died creating the Rosary of 108 beads to seal the Specters' immortality), **Manigoldo of Cancer** (sacrificed himself fighting Thanatos' vessel), **El Cid of Capricorn** (died protecting a comrade), **Sisyphos of Sagittarius** (tore out his own heart), **Kardia of Scorpio** (died from his own heart's heat after defeating Rhadamanthys), **Dégel of Aquarius** (froze himself and Atlantis to seal Poseidon), **Regulus of Leo** (the youngest Gold Saint, disintegrated using the Roar of the Zodiac), **Defteros and Aspros of Gemini** (brotherly sacrifice), and **Sage of Cancer** (former Pope). Only a handful of survivors remain, including Shion, Dohko, Yato, and Yuzuriha [35].

### Saint Seiya Omega (2012–2014)

*Omega* is a 97-episode anime spin-off set 25 years after the original series, following a new generation of Saints protecting Athena. It is **separate from Kurumada's manga continuity** but introduces several armor concepts: **Cloth Stones** (jewels from which Cloths emerge), **Elemental Cosmo** (Fire, Water, Wind, Thunder, Earth, Light, Darkness with a rock-paper-scissors chain), and ultimately **Omega Cloths** (an evolution visually similar to God Cloths). The main protagonist is **Pegasus Kōga**, trained by Ophiuchus Shaina, who defeats the god Mars and later the god Saturn. Legendary original characters appear: **Sagittarius Seiya** (now a Gold Saint), **Libra Shiryu** (father of Dragon Ryūhō, loses all senses but survives), **Andromeda Shun** (a doctor), **Cygnus Hyoga**, **Phoenix Ikki**, and the Old Master **Libra Dohko**. The Steel Saint technology is revived and expanded into an entire army [34][37].

### Saint Seiya: Soul of Gold (2015)

*Soul of Gold* is a 13-episode ONA spin-off and **sequel to the anime-only Asgard chapter**, set in the interval between the Gold Saints' sacrifice at the Wailing Wall and the Bronze Saints' final battle against Hades. All twelve Gold Saints mysteriously revive in Asgard, where they gain **Divine Gold Cloths** (a God Cloth-level Super Mode that drains their life force) and face a new group of God Warriors led by the false god Loki, who wields the Spear of Gungnir. New God Warriors include Gullinbursti Fródi, Nidhogg Fafner, Tanngrisnir Heracles, Eikthyrnir Surtr, Grani Sigmund, Hræsvelgr Baldr, and Garmr Útgarðar. The ending sees the Gold Saints defeat Loki, send their Cloths (with Poseidon's assistance) to help the Bronze Saints in Elysium, and **choose to return to death** [34].

## Conclusion

The Saint Seiya franchise is built on a remarkably consistent armor-and-warrior hierarchy that spans mortal Bronze, Silver, and Gold Cloths; Poseidon's Scales; Hades' Surplices; Asgard's God Robes; and the divine God Cloths and Kamui above them all. The series' central thesis, repeated across every saga, is that **the power of a Saint is not related to the Cloth he wears but to his Cosmo** — Bronze Saints repeatedly overcome Gold Saints and even gods through determination, friendship, and the awakening of higher senses. Each armor class carries its own cosmology: the Cloths of Athena are living artifacts of the Mu Continent's alchemy, the Scales are the Cyclopes' primordial forgeries, the Surplices are tied to the 108 malevolent stars, and the God Robes channel the Norse stars of the Big Dipper. Across the original manga, the 1986 anime, the Hades OVAs, and the spin-offs of *Lost Canvas*, *Next Dimension*, *Omega*, and *Soul of Gold*, the franchise consistently explores how warriors — mortal and divine, heroic and villainous — are defined less by their armor than by the strength of their conviction [1][4][12][13][36].
