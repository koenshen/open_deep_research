# Saint Seiya Franchise: Comprehensive Armor Class and Character Analysis

## Introduction to the Saint Seiya Universe

The *Saint Seiya* franchise, created by Masami Kurumada, is a sprawling mythological epic that blends Greek, Norse, Egyptian, and Hindu cosmology with martial arts and celestial armor. The core premise revolves around warriors called "Saints" who protect the reincarnation of the Greek goddess Athena. These warriors are organized by their armor class, which directly correlates to their power level and status within the hierarchy. The franchise spans multiple series, including the original manga (1986–1990), the original anime (1986–1989), the *Hades* OVA series (2002–2008), *Next Dimension* (2009–present), *Episode G* (2002–2013), *Lost Canvas* (2006–2011), and several feature films.

## Armor Class Hierarchy and Power System

The power system in *Saint Seiya* is built around the concept of the **Seventh Sense** (the sense of the cosmos), the **Eighth Sense** (Arayashiki, the sense of transcendence beyond life and death), and the **Ninth Sense** (attained by gods). Armor classes are:

- **Cloths**: Worn by Saints of Athena. Ranked by material and durability: Bronze (bronze-like alloy), Silver (silver ore), and Gold (Orichalcum, Gammanium, and Star Dust Sand). The highest form is the **God Cloth**, which surpasses divine power.
- **Scales**: Worn by Poseidon's Marina Generals and soldiers. Made from the legendary material Orichalcum.
- **Surplices**: Worn by Hades' Specters. Created from the blood of Hades and infused with dark cosmo.
- **God Robes**: Worn by Olympian gods themselves. The ultimate armor class, virtually indestructible.
- **God Warriors' God Robes**: Worn by the warriors of Asgard, blessed by Odin.
- **Pallasites' Gladiator Cloths**: Worn by the servants of Pallas Athena in *Omega*.
- **Steel Cloths**: Artificial Cloths worn by the Steel Saints in the original anime filler.
- **Angel Cloths**: Worn by Lucifer's Angels in the *Tenkai-hen* film.

# Bronze Saints

The Bronze Saints are the lowest rank in the Sanctuary hierarchy, yet they are the protagonists of the series. They are warriors who have awakened the Seventh Sense and, through their battles, eventually reach the Eighth Sense and attain God Cloths.

## The Five Main Protagonists

### Seiya (Pegasus)

**Power Level**: Awakened Seventh Sense during the Sanctuary arc; later attained the Eighth Sense and the Pegasus God Cloth. By *Next Dimension*, Seiya is a Gold Saint-level combatant. Seiya is the reincarnation of the Pegasus Saint of the previous Holy War, who has fought alongside Athena for centuries.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Pegasus Ryusei Ken* (Pegasus Meteor Fist): A rapid-fire barrage of high-speed punches that strike like a meteor shower.
- *Pegasus Suisei Ken* (Pegasus Comet Fist): A single, concentrated punch that rivals the power of a comet.
- *Pegasus Rolling Crash*: Seiya grabs the opponent and spins them into a devastating throw.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (all arcs), *Hades* (Sanctuary, Inferno, Elysion), *Next Dimension*, *Episode G* (cameo), *Omega* (as a supporting character), *Soul of Gold*, *Legend of Sanctuary* (CGI film), *Tenkai-hen* (film).

**Final Fate**: Survives. In *Hades* Elysion, Seiya and his comrades defeat Hades. In *Next Dimension*, he is gravely wounded by the God of Time but is saved by Athena's intervention. In *Omega*, he is a Golden Saint of Sagittarius and mentor to the new generation.

### Shiryu (Dragon)

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened during the Sanctuary arc. Possesses the strongest physical defense among the Bronze Saints due to his Dragon Cloth's shield. Later attains the Eighth Sense and God Cloth.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Rozan Shoryu Ha* (Rising Dragon Fist): A powerful uppercut that sends the opponent skyward, often combined with the Dragon Cloth's fist.
- *Rozan Hyaku Ryu Ha* (Hundred Dragon Fist): A barrage of dragon-shaped energy blasts.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (all arcs), *Hades*, *Next Dimension*, *Episode G* (as a supporting character).

**Final Fate**: Survives. Becomes a teacher of the Rozan school of martial arts. In *Next Dimension*, he is searching for a cure for Seiya's curse.

### Hyoga (Cygnus)

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. Known for his ice-based techniques. Later attains the Eighth Sense and God Cloth.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Diamond Dust*: A freezing punch that encases the opponent in ice.
- *Kholodnyi Smerch* (Aurora Thunder Attack): A powerful ice tornado.
- *Aurora Execution*: Hyoga's ultimate technique, freezing the opponent to absolute zero.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (all arcs), *Hades*, *Next Dimension*, *Episode G*.

**Final Fate**: Survives. In *Hades*, he freezes the Wailing Wall to allow passage. In *Next Dimension*, he continues his training.

### Shun (Andromeda)

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. Possesses the most versatile Cloth, with chains that can attack and defend. Later reveals immense hidden power, including the ability to use the *Nebula Storm*.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Nebula Chain*: The Andromeda Cloth's chains can be used offensively and defensively.
- *Rolling Defense*: The chains spin to deflect attacks.
- *Nebula Storm*: A devastating cosmic storm that disintegrates the opponent.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (all arcs), *Hades*, *Next Dimension*, *Episode G*.

**Final Fate**: Survives. In *Hades*, he is revealed to be the host of Hades' soul but resists. In *Next Dimension*, he continues his journey.

### Ikki (Phoenix)

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. The strongest of the Bronze Saints, Ikki operates independently. His Phoenix Cloth can regenerate after being destroyed. Later attains the Eighth Sense and God Cloth.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Phoenix Genma Ken* (Phoenix Demon Fist): An illusionary technique that traps the opponent in their worst nightmares.
- *Hoyoku Tensho* (Spread Wings of the Sun): A powerful energy blast.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (all arcs), *Hades*, *Next Dimension*, *Episode G*.

**Final Fate**: Survives. In *Hades*, he sacrifices himself to destroy the Wailing Wall but is revived. In *Next Dimension*, he protects his brother Shun.

# Gold Saints

The Gold Saints are the highest rank of Athena's Saints, guarding the twelve Houses of the Zodiac. They are considered the most powerful warriors in the Sanctuary, each capable of destroying planets with their attacks.

## The Twelve Classic Gold Saints (Sanctuary Arc)

### Aries Mu

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense fully awakened. One of the most skilled Gold Saints, excelling in telekinesis, teleportation, and Cloth repair. He is also the successor to the Pope's position.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Starlight Extinction*: A powerful energy blast that disintegrates the opponent.
- *Stardust Revolution*: Mu's ultimate technique, a cosmic storm of stardust.
- *Crystal Wall*: A defensive barrier of crystal.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension).

**Final Fate**: Survives. Becomes the new Pope after the Hades arc.

### Taurus Aldebaran

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. The most physically powerful Gold Saint.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Great Horn*: A devastating charge that can destroy mountains.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Killed by the Specter Aphrodite (in the anime) or by the Specter Sisyphus (in the manga). Dies protecting the Sanctuary.

### Gemini Saga

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. Considered the strongest Gold Saint alongside Shaka. He has a split personality (good and evil) and can use the *Another Dimension* technique.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Galaxian Explosion*: An energy blast that rivals the Big Bang.
- *Another Dimension*: Sends the opponent to a different dimension.
- *Genro Maō Ken*: A mind-control technique.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension).

**Final Fate**: Dies in the Sanctuary arc after being purified by Athena. Revived in Hades as a Specter, but sacrifices himself to atone for his sins.

### Cancer Deathmask

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. A sadistic Gold Saint who serves the evil Pope.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Sekishiki Meikai Ha*: Sends the opponent's soul to the Underworld.
- *Sekishiki Kōrin*: A wall of tortured souls.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Killed by Shiryu in the Sanctuary arc. Revived in Hades but killed again by Athena.

### Leo Aiolia

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. The most loyal Gold Saint, known for his sense of justice.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Lightning Plasma*: A rapid-fire barrage of lightning bolts.
- *Lightning Bolt*: A concentrated lightning blast.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension, Omega).

**Final Fate**: Survives. Becomes a mentor to the new generation.

### Virgo Shaka

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. Considered the closest to God among the Gold Saints. He is the reincarnation of Buddha.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Tenma Kōfuku*: Opens the opponent's six senses, rendering them helpless.
- *Riku Dō Rinne*: A reincarnation technique that traps the opponent in an endless cycle of death and rebirth.
- *Kurushimi no Harawata*: The ultimate technique, a cosmic destruction.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Dies in the Sanctuary arc after being defeated by Shun. Revived in Hades but sacrifices himself to create the path to the Underworld.

### Libra Dohko

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. The oldest surviving Gold Saint, having fought in the previous Holy War. He is the master of Shiryu.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Rozan Hyaku Ryu Ha*: The Hundred Dragon Fist.
- *Rozan Shoryu Ha*: The Rising Dragon Fist.
- *Libra's Weapons*: Dohko can use the Libra Cloth's various weapons (swords, tridents, etc.).

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension, Lost Canvas).

**Final Fate**: Dies in the Hades arc after destroying the Wailing Wall. Revived in Next Dimension.

### Scorpio Milo

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. A master of the Scarlet Needle technique.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Scarlet Needle (Antares)*: A series of venomous needles that paralyze the opponent.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension).

**Final Fate**: Survives.

### Sagittarius Aiolos

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. The former Pope candidate who was killed by Saga. He protects Athena and the Cloth.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Sagittarius's Arrow*: A powerful arrow that can destroy anything.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades, Next Dimension).

**Final Fate**: Dies in the past. His spirit appears to guide the Bronze Saints.

### Capricorn Shura

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. A master swordsman whose body becomes a blade.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Excalibur*: A sword strike from his arm.
- *Jumping Stone*: A technique that allows him to jump high.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Killed by Shiryu in the Sanctuary arc. Revived in Hades but sacrifices himself.

### Aquarius Camus

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. A master of absolute zero ice techniques. He is the teacher of Hyoga.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Aurora Execution*: Freezes the opponent to absolute zero.
- *Diamond Dust*: A freezing punch.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Killed by Hyoga in the Sanctuary arc. Revived in Hades but sacrifices himself.

### Pisces Aphrodite

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. A narcissistic Gold Saint who uses poison roses.

**Signature Techniques**:
- *Royal Demon Rose*: A poisonous rose that drains the opponent's life.
- *Piranha Rose*: A rose that shreds the opponent.
- *Bloody Rose*: The ultimate technique, a rose that turns the opponent's blood into a weapon.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Sanctuary, Hades).

**Final Fate**: Killed by Shun in the Sanctuary arc. Revived in Hades but killed again.

## Additional Gold Saints

### Gemini Kanon (Saga's Brother)

**Power Level**: Seventh Sense awakened. Equivalent to Saga in power.

**Signature Techniques**: Same as Saga: *Galaxian Explosion*, *Another Dimension*, *Genro Maō Ken*.

**Key Appearances**: Original manga/anime (Poseidon, Hades, Next Dimension).

**Final Fate**: Survives. Redeems himself by helping the Bronze Saints in the Poseidon arc and Hades arc.

# Conclusion

The *Saint Seiya* franchise presents a richly layered hierarchy of warriors and armor classes, each with distinct power levels, techniques, and narrative arcs. The Bronze Saints, initially the lowest rank, transcend their limitations through the power of the Seventh and Eighth Senses, ultimately attaining God Cloths to challenge divine beings. The Gold Saints represent the pinnacle of mortal power, while the various antagonist groups—Marina Generals, Specters, God Warriors, Pallasites, and Angels—provide formidable challenges that expand the mythological scope of the universe. Spin-off series like *Lost Canvas* and *Episode G* enrich the lore by exploring past and alternate timelines, while *Next Dimension* continues the classical saga. The franchise's enduring appeal lies in its fusion of mythology, martial arts, and the theme of courage overcoming impossible odds.
