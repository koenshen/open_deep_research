# AI/ML Medical Devices in Radiology First Cleared by the FDA (August 2024 – August 2026)

## 1. Scope and Regulatory Context

The FDA's Center for Devices and Radiological Health (CDRH) maintains the official **"Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices"** list, which identifies AI-enabled medical devices authorized for marketing in the United States [1]. The list is updated periodically, sorts devices by date of final decision, and links each submission number to the corresponding 510(k), De Novo, or PMA record. As of the March 30, 2026 update, the list contained **1,524 total AI-enabled device entries, of which 1,163 (76.31%) were radiology devices** [3]. Radiology has dominated FDA AI authorizations for three decades, accounting for **1,094 of 1,430 cleared AI devices (76.5%)** between September 1995 and December 2025 [4].

Clearance volumes set records during the window:

- **2025:** 295 AI/ML 510(k) clearances from 221 unique manufacturers — a record; **211 (71.5%) were radiology**; median FDA review time 142 days; 30 devices (10.2%) authorized with Predetermined Change Control Plans (PCCPs) [2].
- **2024:** 253 AI/ML clearances, with radiology representing ~74% [5].
- **Q1 2026:** 92 AI-enabled devices cleared, 69 (75%) for radiology [3].
- **Cumulative leaders at end-2025:** GE HealthCare (120), Siemens Healthineers (89), Philips (50), Canon (45), United Imaging (38), Aidoc (31) [5].

The FDA explicitly cautions that the AI/ML list "is not a comprehensive resource of AI-enabled medical devices" and that authorizations lacking published decision summaries are incorporated into subsequent updates [1]. For verification, the FDA's **510(k) database** (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm) and **De Novo database** (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm) provide authoritative clearance records. A JAMA Network Open systematic review of 950 AI/ML devices authorized through June 2024 found 76% were radiology, 97% were cleared via 510(k), and only 5% of radiology devices had undergone prospective testing [6].

This report lists radiology AI/ML devices that received their **first** FDA clearance — either an initial 510(k) clearance or a De Novo grant — between roughly August 2024 and August 2026. Devices that are updates or modifications of previously cleared products are excluded (see Section 6). Each entry provides the FDA source and a med-tech news article, or an authoritative alternative where no news coverage could be identified.

## 3. De Novo Grants (First-of-Kind Authorizations)

### 3.1 Clairity Breast (Allix5) — Clairity, Inc.

- **Clearance:** De Novo Grant, **May 30, 2025** (request received Sep 6, 2024); DEN240047 [7]
- **Description:** The **first FDA-authorized AI platform for 5-year breast cancer risk prediction**. It analyzes bilateral screening mammograms (Hologic Lorad Selenia / Selenia Dimensions FFDM) to generate a 5-year risk score. Classified as Class II under special controls, including clinical performance validation testing, software verification/validation, labeling requirements, and a post-market performance management plan. It is prescription-only and is not intended to diagnose or detect breast cancer [7][10].
- **Validation:** Trained on millions of images and validated on more than 77,000 mammograms from five geographically distinct screening centers; 85% of women diagnosed with breast cancer have no family history [8][10]. Commercial launch was planned for late 2025, with the first partnership at Beth Israel Deaconess (Boston). In 2026, the NCCN guidelines added a 5-year AI mammogram risk threshold of ≥1.7% [10].
- **FDA link(s):** De Novo classification order PDF [7]; FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Diagnostic Imaging (Jun 2, 2025) [8]; Pharmacy Times [9]; BCRF [10]

### 3.2 Delivery Date AI — Ultrasound AI

- **Clearance:** De Novo Grant, **Feb 11, 2026** (FDA decision; received Mar 17, 2025); DEN250007 [11][12]
- **Description:** Cloud-based software using deep-learning neural networks to predict a delivery date from standard ultrasound images without manual biometry ("image-first" approach). The FDA record classifies it as a "radiological software system for delivery date prediction" (Regulation 892.8200, product code SHE, Radiology panel) [11]. A 2025 study in the *Journal of Maternal-Fetal and Neonatal Medicine* (PAIR study: 5,714 women, 19,940 exams, 877,141 images) reported R² = 0.92 for delivery date prediction and a mean absolute error of 19.99 days [13][14].
- **FDA link(s):** De Novo database entry [11]; classification order PDF [12]; FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Diagnostic Imaging (Mar 2, 2026) [13]; Contemporary OB/GYN (Mar 2, 2026) [14]

### 3.3 neuropacs — Automated Imaging Diagnostics (neuropacs Corp.)

- **Clearance:** De Novo Grant, **Apr 3, 2026** (received Dec 4, 2024); DEN240071 [15]
- **Description:** AI-powered MRI diagnostic aid for Parkinsonian syndromes — it "classifies diffusion-MRI patterns for Parkinson's disease, MSA parkinsonian variant, and PSP." The FDA tightly bounded the claim, noting it supplements neurological assessment and cannot stand alone. The company reports up to 96–98% precision in distinguishing Parkinson's disease from MSAp and PSP, using Free-Water Imaging (FWI) diffusion-MRI, developed from University of Florida / UF Health Norman Fixel Institute research, with 21 sites in the most recent clinical trial [15][16].
- **FDA link(s):** De Novo database entry [15]; FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** No independent med-tech outlet article was found for this clearance; the manufacturer's press release is provided as the authoritative alternative [16].

### 3.4 Pulsenmore ES — Pulsenmore

- **Clearance:** De Novo marketing approval, **Nov 3, 2025** [17]
- **Description:** Home-use prenatal ultrasound platform. A cradle connects to a smartphone with an app providing step-by-step guidance; ultrasound video clips are transmitted securely for remote clinician interpretation. It has been used for 200,000+ patient scans in Israel, Europe, Australia, and Brazil, with a phased U.S. launch targeting early 2026 — positioned to help women in "maternity care deserts" (35% of U.S. counties) [17].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1] (the De Novo number was not identified in the available sources)
- **News coverage:** Diagnostic Imaging [17]

## 4. 510(k) Clearances — First-Time Devices

### 4.1 a2z-Unified-Triage — a2z Radiology AI

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Nov 24, 2025** (received Jul 30, 2025); K252366 [20]
- **Description:** Described as the first-of-its-kind single device that simultaneously flags and prioritizes **seven urgent findings on abdomen-pelvis CT**: small bowel obstruction, acute cholecystitis, acute pancreatitis, acute diverticulitis, hydronephrosis, free air, and unruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm. For five of the seven conditions, the company says it is bringing AI triage to the U.S. market for the first time. The Boston-based startup was co-founded by Pranav Rajpurkar, PhD (Harvard) and Samir Rajpurkar, and raised $4.5M in seed funding. The FDA record confirms product code QAS (subsequent QFM) with PCCP authorized [20][21][22].
- **FDA link(s):** 510(k) database entry K252366 [20]; FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** AuntMinnie (Nov 26, 2025) [21]; Imaging Technology News (Nov 26, 2025) [22]

### 4.2 eyonis LCS — Median Technologies

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Feb 9, 2026** [23][24]
- **Description:** Described as the **first FDA-cleared end-to-end AI/ML-powered CADe/CADx SaMD for lung cancer screening**. It analyzes low-dose CT (LDCT) for detection and characterization of parenchymal pulmonary nodules, with manufacturer-testing performance of 93.3% sensitivity, 92.4% specificity, 99.9% negative predictive value, and a false-positive rate of 1 per 1,000. It integrates into PACS and has been assigned CMS Category III CPT codes 0721T/0722T. A Tempus AI distribution partnership was announced Feb 12, 2026 [23][24].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** AuntMinnie (Feb 9, 2026) [23]; Median Technologies press release [24]

### 4.3 Butterfly Gestational Age Tool — Butterfly Network

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Mar 30, 2026** [25][26]
- **Description:** A fully automated Gestational Age Tool integrated into Butterfly's handheld ultrasound — described as the **first FDA-cleared blind-sweep ultrasound AI tool for estimating gestational age**. The workflow takes under two minutes: enter fundal height, apply gel, and perform guided sweeps. It is intended for patients at 16–37 weeks, was trained on 21M+ images using deep-learning models from UNC Chapel Hill, and has already been deployed in Malawi and Uganda with Gates Foundation grant support [25][26].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list (listed as "Butterfly Gestational Age Tool," Radiology, Mar 2026) [1]
- **News coverage:** Butterfly Network press release (Mar 30, 2026) [25]; MassDevice [26]

### 4.4 BrightHeart B-Right Screen — BrightHeart

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **November 2024** — the company's first FDA clearance [27][28]
- **Description:** AI software that flags structural markers suggestive of congenital heart defects (CHDs) during second-trimester fetal ultrasound exams. It was built on Meta's DINOv2 foundation model and reports 98.7% AI sensitivity for severe CHD indicators and 97.7% specificity. BrightHeart subsequently received a second clearance (real-time tablet access, May 2025) and a third for B-Right Views (May 14, 2025), and was one of the first companies to receive PCCP approval [27][28].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** AuntMinnie (May 14, 2025 — explicitly notes the initial Nov 2024 clearance) [27]; BrightHeart press release [28]

### 4.5 DeepEcho Fetal Ultrasound Analysis Platform — DeepEcho

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Jun 20, 2025** (company news page lists May 31, 2025) [29][30]
- **Description:** AI-enabled software for fetal ultrasound assessment that "provides biometric measurement calculations through automated segmentation of anatomical landmarks." Clinicians collect fetal biometry images/planes with three short video loops; the company reports "greater than 95 percent accuracy" in fetal ultrasound evaluations. The company has offices in New York, NY and Rabat, Morocco [29][30].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Diagnostic Imaging (Jun 20, 2025) [29]; AccessNewswire [30]

### 4.6 ViTAA AiORTA Plan — ViTAA Medical Solutions

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Nov 12, 2025**; K250337 [31][32]
- **Description:** A fully automated, cloud-based aortic surgery planning SaMD — the company's first FDA clearance — providing automated aneurysm segmentation, centerline extraction, diameter/angulation measurements, and volumetric modeling. The Montreal-based company's platform is part of a broader AiORTA product family (Maps and Watch in clinical studies) [31][32]. Note: the FDA AI/ML list shows an entry for "AiORTA - Plan v2.0" dated Mar 30, 2026, which may reflect a subsequent version of this device [1].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA 510(k) database entry for K250337 (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K250337); FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** ViTAA press release (Nov 12, 2025) [31]; Medical Device Network (Nov 13, 2025) [32]

### 4.7 Naitive OsteoSight — Naitive Technologies

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Sep 11, 2025** [33][34]
- **Description:** AI-enabled opportunistic bone mineral density (BMD) assessment from standard hip/pelvis X-rays in patients aged 50+, flagging low-BMD risk. It holds FDA Breakthrough Device designation (2023) and is the company's first FDA clearance. The London-based company is led by CEO Dr. Will Briggs and is under evaluation at the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital NHS Trust [33][34].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** PRNewswire (Sep 11, 2025) [33]; Medical Device Network [34]

### 4.8 Bunkerhill Health Portfolio (AVC, BMD, MAC, AAQ) — Bunkerhill Health

Bunkerhill Health received multiple distinct first-time 510(k) clearances during the window, each for a different AI algorithm developed through a consortium of 25+ academic medical centers and available within the Carebricks platform:

- **Bunkerhill AVC (aortic valve calcification)** — 510(k) announced **Jan 29, 2025**. Described as the first FDA-cleared AI solution that detects and quantifies aortic valve calcification on routine non-gated, non-contrast chest CT [35].
- **Bunkerhill BMD (bone density)** — 510(k) announced **Apr 10, 2025**. AI-powered opportunistic bone density assessment from existing non-contrast abdominal CT scans in adults aged 30+, without additional hardware, calibration phantoms, or radiation [36].
- **Bunkerhill MAC (mitral annular calcification)** — 510(k) announced **Oct 8, 2025**. Described as the first AI algorithm to detect and quantify mitral annular calcification on routine, non-gated chest CT scans; data from seven consortium sites (Emory, Thomas Jefferson, UCSF) were included for clearance [37][38].
- **Bunkerhill AAQ (abdominal aortic quantification)** — 510(k) (exact date not specified in sources). Described as the first AI-powered algorithm that automatically measures maximum abdominal aortic diameter on routine abdominal CT scans with or without IV contrast, developed at Stanford [39].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Company press releases: AVC [35]; BMD [36]; MAC [37][38]; AAQ [39]

### 4.9 RevealAI-Lung — Precision Medical Ventures, Inc. dba Revealdx

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Jan 30, 2026** (decision; received Jun 10, 2025); K251769 [18][19]
- **Description:** Computer-Assisted Diagnostic Software for Lesions Suspicious for Cancer (product code POK; Regulation 892.2060; Radiology panel; Traditional 510(k); decision "Substantially Equivalent (SESE)") [18].
- **FDA link(s):** 510(k) database entry K251769 [18]; decision summary PDF [19]
- **News coverage:** No med-tech news coverage was found for this device; the FDA database entry is provided as the authoritative source [18].

### 4.10 Provect AI 3D Intraoperative Imaging — Provect AI

- **Clearance:** 510(k), announced **Jul 16, 2026** [40][41]
- **Description:** Software that generates **3D intraoperative images from standard 2D C-arm X-ray data** (no CT scanner needed) for spine and orthopedic image-guided procedures, with DICOM export and physics-aware reconstruction. It is the first clearance for the Palo Alto-based startup, which simultaneously raised $7M led by Arteria Capital and ValueStream Ventures [40][41].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** AuntMinnie (Jul 17, 2026) [40]; Provect AI press release [41]

### 4.11 Viz Subdural Plus — Viz.ai

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Jun 12, 2025** [42]
- **Description:** Described as the first FDA clearance for automated subdural collection quantification — measuring volume, thickness, and midline shift of subdural hemorrhages on non-contrast CT. It is part of the Viz.ai One platform, deployed across 1,800+ hospitals and health systems in the U.S. and Europe. Chronic subdural hematoma is projected to reach ~60,000 new U.S. cases annually by 2030 [42].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Viz.ai press release (Jun 12, 2025) [42]

### 4.12 qXR-Detect — Qure.ai

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Feb 26, 2026** [43][44]
- **Description:** AI-powered computer-aided detection (CAD) for chest X-rays covering **six indication areas**: lung, pleura, mediastinum/hila and heart, bone, hardware, and others, with bounding boxes and region-of-interest labels. It is described as the **only chest X-ray CAD device cleared by the FDA with a Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP)**, bringing Qure.ai to 26 FDA-cleared indications across nine products for X-ray and CT [43][44].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** AuntMinnie (Feb 27, 2026) [43]; Qure.ai newsroom [44]

## 5. Boundary Cases (Imaging AI at the Edges of Radiology)

### 5.1 AISAP CARDIO — AISAP

- **Clearance:** 510(k), **Aug 22, 2024** [45]
- **Description:** A cloud-based AI-enabled point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) platform combining four CADx modules for valvular pathologies and eight key measurements (LVEF, RV FAC, IVC diameter, etc.). It was trained on 24M+ echocardiography video clips and is the company's first FDA clearance. This is a cardiac POCUS device rather than a radiology-department product, but it is included here as an imaging-AI first clearance at the start of the window [45].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** Diagnostic Imaging (Aug 22, 2024) [45]

### 5.2 iPredict-DR — iHealthScreen

- **Clearance:** 510(k), announced **Jul 8, 2026**; K253704 [46]
- **Description:** AI software to automatically detect more-than-mild diabetic retinopathy (mtmDR) from retinal fundus images (iCare DRSplus camera). It is the company's first clearance; additional clearances are planned for AMD, glaucoma, and hypertensive retinopathy. This is retinal/ophthalmology imaging, at the boundary of the radiology field [46].
- **FDA link(s):** FDA 510(k) database entry for K253704 (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K253704); FDA AI/ML-enabled device list [1]
- **News coverage:** BusinessWire (Jul 8, 2026) [46]
