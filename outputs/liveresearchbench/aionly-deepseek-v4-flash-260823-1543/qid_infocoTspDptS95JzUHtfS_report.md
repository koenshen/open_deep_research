# S&P 500 Consumer-Facing Corporate Rebrands: January 1, 2023 – August 23, 2026

## Executive Summary

This report identifies S&P 500 consumer-facing companies that executed a full corporate re-brand — defined as a new logo and/or new brand tagline — between January 1, 2023 and August 23, 2026. The S&P 500 membership is considered as of August 24, 2026.

The research identified **12 qualifying companies/rebrands**, with varying degrees of completeness of the requested data fields:

| # | Company (Brand) | Rebrand Type | Launch Date | New Logo? | New Tagline? |

| 2 | Johnson & Johnson | New logo & visual identity | Sep 14, 2023 | Yes | Yes ("Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine" / "MedTech") |
| 3 | Kenvue | New corporate brand (logo & name) | May 4, 2023 (IPO/launch) | Yes | Yes ("Realize the Extraordinary Power of Everyday Care") |

(3) For several companies, a dedicated independent design-industry article could not be confirmed; trade-press coverage is listed instead.

## Company Profiles

### 1. Pepsi (PepsiCo Inc.)

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_pepsi.png` — the 2014 "Big Bold Blue" Pepsi Globe with lowercase wordmark, in use June 2, 2014 – Aug 23, 2023 [4].
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_pepsi.png` — the 2023 Pepsi Globe with centered uppercase black wordmark, electric blue base, and new "Pepsi pulse" element [1][4].
- **Launch date:** Announced March 28, 2023; launched in the US on August 23, 2023 (coinciding with the brand's 125th anniversary); global rollout through 2024 [1][4].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No official brand-guidelines PDF was verified on a Pepsi/PepsiCo domain.** The closest official equivalents are the [PepsiCo Media Library](https://www.pepsico.com/en/media) (official logos, launch kits, brand assets) and the PepsiCo Foodservice Asset Library. Unofficial mirrors of the pre-rebrand 2014 "Big Bold Blue" guidelines exist on Slideshare/Scribd but do not cover the 2023 identity [5][7][8].
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Brand New (UnderConsideration): ["New Logo, Identity, and Packaging for Pepsi done In-house"](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_identity_and_packaging_for_pepsi_done_in_house.php) [2]
  - Dezeen: ["Dezeen's top 10 rebrands and logo redesigns of 2023"](https://www.dezeen.com/2023/12/15/rebrands-logo-redesigns-2023-review) — ranked Pepsi as the #1 rebrand of 2023 [3].

**Key facts:** This was Pepsi's first logo update in 14 years. The design features a bold black typeface (nod to 1960s branding), a signature "pulse" element evoking the "ripple, pop and fizz" of Pepsi-Cola, and an updated palette introducing electric blue and black — with black signaling the brand's commitment to Pepsi Zero Sugar. Mauro Porcini, PepsiCo SVP & Chief Design Officer: "We designed the new brand identity to connect future generations with our brand's heritage" [1].

### 2. Johnson & Johnson

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_johnson_johnson.png` — the red script/signature logo based on co-founder James Wood Johnson's signature, in use from 1887 to September 2023 [6][8][10].
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_johnson_johnson.png` — a custom sans-serif wordmark designed by Wolff Olins, with each letter "drawn in one pen stroke," a redesigned prominent ampersand, a refreshed "Passion Red" color, and a short-form "J&J" version for digital/social use [6][7].
- **Launch date:** September 14, 2023, announced via official press release; rollout across materials, packaging, and assets occurred over time [6].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No official brand-guidelines PDF was verified on jnj.com.** The [official press release](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-marks-new-era-as-global-healthcare-company-with-updated-visual-identity) is the primary official source; logo downloads are gated behind J&J's internal brand portal [6][8]. An unofficial mirror ("J&J Brand Guidelines Overview") exists on Scribd [9].
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Creative Review: ["Johnson & Johnson unveils new logo and visual identity"](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/johnson-johnson-new-logo) — discusses the replacement of the 136-year-old script, the Wolff Olins design, the "blandification" debate, and the link to the Kenvue spinoff [7].

**Key facts:** The rebrand united J&J's pharma and medtech segments under one master brand — Janssen became "Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine" and the medtech segment became "Johnson & Johnson MedTech." The refreshed bright red was chosen "reflecting urgency and responsiveness to health challenges." The change generated significant social-media debate, with some commentators arguing the company discarded its most valuable visual asset [6][7][10].

### 3. Kenvue Inc. (formerly Johnson & Johnson Consumer Healthcare)

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_kenvue.png` — prior to the spin-off, the business operated as "Johnson & Johnson Consumer Inc." under the J&J script logo [30].
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_kenvue.png` — the Kenvue wordmark and green "K" monogram developed by Wolff Olins, balancing "scientific precision (rectangular geometry) with the warmth of care (rounded edges)" [10][11].
- **Launch date:** The brand name "Kenvue" was announced September 28, 2022; the full corporate identity launched with the company's IPO on May 4, 2023, and the separation from J&J completed in July 2023. Kenvue joined the S&P 500 on August 25, 2023, replacing Advance Auto Parts [13][14][15].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No official brand-guidelines PDF was verified on kenvue.com.** The company's [media/newsroom page](https://www.kenvue.com/media) is the closest official asset source [13].
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Brand New (UnderConsideration): ["New Logo for Kenvue by Wolff Olins"](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_for_kenvue_by_wolff_olins.php) — posted October 19, 2022, covering the identity introduction [11].
  - Wolff Olins case study: ["Work — Kenvue — Verbal Identity and more"](https://wolffolins.com/work/kenvue) [10].

**Key facts:** Kenvue owns a portfolio of iconic consumer-health brands including Tylenol, Band-Aid, Listerine, Neutrogena, Aveeno, and Johnson's Baby. The brand purpose is "Realize the Extraordinary Power of Everyday Care." The Wolff Olins identity had to create brand equity from scratch for a $41 billion company with 100+ years of heritage but no brand recognition of its own. Brand architecture shifted from J&J's "monolithic" strategy to an "independent" strategy, with product brands no longer carrying a direct parent-company association [10][13].

### 4. Walmart Inc.

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_walmart.png` — the 2008 wordmark ("Walmart" in one word) with the original yellow Spark [16][20].
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_walmart.png` — the 2025 wordmark "inspired by Sam Walton's classic trucker hat" with a custom typeface ("Everyday Sans"), a refined Spark icon, and an updated "True Blue / Spark Yellow" palette [16][17][19].
- **Launch date:** Announced January 13, 2025. The rollout actually began in October 2024 with Store 4108 in Springdale, Arkansas; the identity launched officially on Walmart.com, the app, marketing assets, and the corporate site in January 2025 [16][20].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No downloadable PDF was verified, but Walmart operates an official web-based brand center:** [brandcenter.walmart.com](https://brandcenter.walmart.com), with dedicated pages for the [brand foundation](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-foundation), [wordmark](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/wordmark), [color](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/color), and typography assets [17].
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Brand New (UnderConsideration): ["New Logo and Identity for Walmart by Jones Knowles Ritchie and Walmart Creative"](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_and_identity_for_walmart_by_jones_knowles_ritchie_and_in_house.php) — posted January 15, 2025, subtitled "Let the Sparks Fly" [18].
  - Jones Knowles Ritchie case study: [JKR — Walmart](https://www.jkrglobal.com/work/walmart) [19].

**Key facts:** The refresh was led by JKR and the Walmart Creative Studio. It was Walmart's first logo redesign in nearly two decades. The wordmark is "a custom piece of artwork, not just the word 'Walmart' in Everyday Sans" per the official brand center. The new palette retains True Blue (now paired with Spark Yellow) and the Spark was refined "to be as dynamic as the people Walmart serves." Public reaction was largely negative on social media — commentators noted the similarity to the old logo and criticized a rumored $1.25 million budget; design commentators including Fast Company's Mark Wilson called it "uninspired," while others praised the new blue and typeface. The brand center explicitly retains the purpose "Save money. Live better." [16][17][18][19].

### 5. The Coca-Cola Company (Coca-Cola brand)

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_coca_cola.png` — the pre-July 2026 identity built around the Spencerian script, red disc, and Dynamic Ribbon. Note: the core script logo itself was not radically changed [21][22].
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_coca_cola.png` — the July 2026 global visual identity system that amplifies the signature assets (bold red/white palette, Dynamic Ribbon, Arden Square, Spencerian Script) for consistency across 200+ markets; Coca-Cola Zero Sugar received distinct cues (larger "Zero Sugar" text, black Dynamic Ribbon, black caps) [21][22][23].
- **Launch date:** July 20, 2026, with rollout beginning across Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia [21].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No public brand-guidelines PDF was verified.** The [official media-center announcement](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/coca-cola-sharpens-its-identity-under-one-bold-visual-system) describes a new immersive "Brand Center and Design Intelligence tools" for internal teams and agency partners, but no public URL or PDF was surfaced [21].
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Brand New (UnderConsideration): ["New Identity and Packaging for Coca-Cola"](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_identity_and_packaging_for_coca_cola.php) — subtitled "Soda'mn Good," posted July 20, 2026, with 253 comments [22].
  - Creative Review: ["Coca-Cola sharpens identity with global design system built for scale"](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/coca-cola-sharpens-identity-with-global-design-system-built-for-scale) [23].
  - PRINT Magazine: ["Coca-Cola Unveils a New Global Brand Identity"](https://www.printmag.com/branding-identity-design/coca-cola-unveils-a-new-global-brand-identity) [24].

**Key facts:** Developed by JKR with packaging by The Superultrarare and a proprietary typeface by Brody Associates, the system is based on the strategic principle to "make Coca-Cola more Coca-Cola." JKR's Tosh Hall: "Think of it as a love letter to what makes Coca-Cola, Coca-Cola." Coca-Cola's Rapha Abreu likened the work to "designing the operating system rather than every application." Some consumers criticized the Zero Sugar redesign as "indistinguishable" from Original Taste cans [21][23].

### 6. Amazon.com, Inc.

- **Old logo file name:** `old_logo_amazon.png` — the pre-2025 wordmark with the smile-arrow (in use since 2000, refined 2012).
- **New logo file name:** `new_logo_amazon.png` — the May 2025 update by design agency Koto and Amazon's in-house team (Amazon XCM): a new "Ember Modern" typeface, a deeper and more emphatic smile, an expanded arrow shaft and point, and a more vibrant orange ("Smile Orange") [25][26][27].
- **Launch date:** May 2025 (reported by Dezeen and multiple industry roundups) [25][26][27].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No official brand-guidelines PDF was verified on an Amazon domain.**
- **Independent design-industry article:**
  - Dezeen: ["Dezeen's top rebrands and logo redesigns of 2025"](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/12/02/rebrands-logo-2025) — covers the Koto collaboration in detail [25].
  - Punch: [Branding: The top 25 Rebrands of 2025](https://www.brandsthatpunch.com/blogs/top-25-rebrands-of-2025) — lists Amazon's May 2025 refresh as one of the year's top rebrands [26].
  - Looka: [The Biggest Logo Redesigns of 2025 (So Far)](https://looka.com/blog/logo-redesigns-2025) [27].

**Key facts:** Amazon's first logo update in two decades. Koto's stated intent: "Our update puts greater emphasis not on the arrow, but on a deeper and more emphatic smile, reflecting Amazon's mission to delight customers." The refresh unified 50+ sub-brands under one design system. It faced public criticism, with some critics calling the orange generic or missing the A-to-Z reference, and criticism tied to Amazon's labor record [25][26][27].

- **Launch date:** November 19, 2024 — shareholders "overwhelmingly approved" the name change, which became effective upon filing an amendment to the company's certificate of incorporation with the state of New Jersey [28].
- **Official brand-guidelines PDF link:** **No official brand-guidelines PDF was verified.**
- **Independent design-industry article:** **No dedicated design-industry publication article was found in the research.** Closest coverage:
  - Mass Market Retailers (trade press): ["Campbell Soup Co. rebrands as the Campbell's Company"](https://massmarketretailers.com/campbell-soup-co-rebrands-as-the-campbells-company-to-reflect-its-expanded-portfolio) [29].

The change reflects a strategic pivot toward snacks (snack sales grew 13% vs. soup's 3% in the prior year) [28][29][30].

## Companies Investigated but Not Qualifying

The following companies were investigated and either did not meet the S&P 500 membership requirement, did not rebrand within the window, or executed only a sub-brand/format change:

- **Kellanova (formerly Kellogg Company):** Executed a major corporate rebrand on October 2, 2023, when Kellogg Company renamed itself Kellanova after splitting off WK Kellogg Co. However, Mars completed its acquisition of Kellanova on December 11, 2025, and the shares were delisted from the NYSE — so Kellanova is **not** an S&P 500 member as of August 24, 2026 [41][42].
- **Cracker Barrel Old Country Store:** Rolled out a redesigned logo and "All the More" rebrand on August 19, 2025, but reversed it on August 26, 2025, after massive backlash. The company is **not** in the S&P 500 — it was moved from the S&P MidCap 400 to the S&P SmallCap 600 in March 2023 and remains a small-cap (~$1.2 billion market cap) [43][44][45].
- **Advance Auto Parts:** Changed its logo to a black/white/yellow scheme circa 2023–2024, but was removed from the S&P 500 on August 25, 2023 (replaced by Kenvue) and is now in the S&P SmallCap 600 [15][40].
- **Instacart (Maplebear Inc.):** Executed its carrot-logo rebrand in **2022** (outside the window). It is not in the S&P 500; it joined the S&P MidCap 400 in January 2025 [46][47].
- **Yum! Brands / Pizza Hut:** Pizza Hut unveiled a refreshed international logo in 2025 (UK, Canada, South Africa, Australia), but this is a sub-brand refresh — Yum! Brands' corporate identity did not change, and the US market was not affected [49].
- **Alphabet/Google:** A minor "soft-focus" G-icon gradient update occurred in 2025, but this is an incremental icon tweak rather than a full corporate re-brand [25].
- **Nike:** Introduced a "Podium" global design system in July 2025, but the Swoosh and wordmark were unchanged [26].
- **Warner Bros. Discovery / Max:** The HBO Max-to-Max rebrand was a streaming-service/sub-brand renaming, not a corporate identity change for the S&P 500 parent [49].
- **PayPal (2024) and Verizon (2024):** Both executed notable logo redesigns within the window and are consumer-facing; however, they fall outside the brief's consumer-retail/CPG focus (fintech and telecom, respectively) and were not confirmed as qualifying under the brief's scope [48][49].
- **No rebrand found in window:** Target, Kroger, McDonald's, Starbucks, Dollar General, Nike (logo), Nike brand tagline, Delta, United, American Airlines, Marriott, Hilton, and other major B2C S&P 500 names were checked; none executed a corporate logo or tagline change within the window [48].

## Data Completeness Notes

1. **Brand-guidelines PDFs:** The brief requested a direct link to a brand-guidelines PDF hosted on the company's own domain. **No such PDF was verified for any company on this list.** Brand guidelines for most major corporations are gated behind internal portals or distributed only to approved agencies. The closest official equivalents are noted per company (e.g., Walmart's web-based brand center, Tyson's Enterprise Brand Guide web page, PepsiCo's media library). This is a genuine data gap that could not be resolved through public sources.
2. **Logo file names:** No literal logo filenames could be verified from official asset-download pages in any search. The conventional filenames provided (`old_logo_*.png` / `new_logo_*.png`) are formatted per the brief's request and describe the actual before/after logos accurately.
3. **Design-industry articles:** For Pepsi, J&J, Kenvue, Walmart, Coca-Cola, Amazon, Colgate-Palmolive, Domino's, and SiriusXM, articles from reputable design-industry publications (Brand New/UnderConsideration, Dezeen, Creative Review, PRINT, Punch) were confirmed. For Campbell's, Best Buy, and Tyson Foods, no dedicated design-industry publication article was found; coverage from trade publications (Mass Market Retailers, ADWEEK, Marketing Dive, Talk Business & Politics) is cited instead.
4. **Best Buy nuance:** Best Buy's rebrand qualifies only under the "new brand tagline" prong of the definition, since the logo itself was not changed. It is included for completeness but flagged accordingly.
5. **Coca-Cola nuance:** The July 2026 change is a visual-identity-system overhaul rather than a new logo; the Spencerian script itself was retained and amplified. It qualifies because the overall brand identity system (logo presentation, packaging, Zero Sugar treatment) was substantially renewed.
