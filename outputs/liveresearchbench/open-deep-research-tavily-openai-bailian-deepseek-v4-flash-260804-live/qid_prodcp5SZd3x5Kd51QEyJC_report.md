# Comprehensive Comparison of Premium Over-Ear Wireless Headphones (August 2026)

## Introduction

This report provides a detailed comparison of five leading over-ear wireless headphones as of August 4, 2026: **Bose QuietComfort 45**, **Bose QuietComfort Ultra**, **Sony WH-1000XM5**, **Apple AirPods Max**, and **Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless**. The analysis covers active noise cancellation (ANC) performance, sound quality and audio accuracy, comfort and build materials, battery life, and current US market pricing. All data is sourced from reputable audio review publications, manufacturer websites, and verified e-commerce platforms.

## Detailed Analysis

### 1. Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Performance

#### Bose QuietComfort 45
The QC45 delivers excellent ANC performance, earning PCMag's Editors' Choice designation. SoundGuys measured up to 50 dB of attenuation at certain frequencies, outperforming the Sennheiser PXC 550-II by nearly 10 dB [1][2]. In comparison with the QC Ultra, the QC45 reduced noise by 20-30 dB, while the Ultra achieved up to 50 dB [3]. Crutchfield's reviewer noted that "in terms of absolute 'quiet,' the QC 45s are arguably the best ever — regardless of brand" [27]. The headphones feature Quiet Mode (full ANC) and Aware Mode (transparency) but cannot turn off ANC wirelessly — only via the 3.5mm cable with the headphones powered off [51].

#### Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen)
The 2nd Gen QC Ultra improves upon the already excellent 1st Gen, which reduced perceived loudness by 85% [4]. SoundGuys measured the 2nd Gen at 87% noise reduction, on par with other top-tier ANC headphones [5]. The 2nd Gen adds ActiveSense adaptive noise cancellation, which reduces loud, sudden noises in Transparency mode, and a new Cinema Mode for spatialized video content [4][6]. The official Bose product page states: "Next-gen QC Ultra Headphones deliver even more luxe listening with improved noise cancellation" [52]. YouTube reviewer Raminmark rated the ANC 9.5/10, calling it "virtually silent with no hissing" [53].

#### Sony WH-1000XM5
The XM5 features industry-leading ANC, described by SoundGuys as "the king of the ANC pack extends its reign" [7]. HeadphonesAddict measured an average noise reduction of 29.48 dBA, with class-leading performance in the midrange [8]. The headphones use eight microphones controlled by the Integrated Processor V1 and HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1, with features including Auto NC Optimizer, Atmospheric Pressure Optimizing, and Quick Attention mode [9][54]. SoundGuys notes that the XM5 "significantly improves noise attenuation both passively and actively, reducing low-frequency sounds (engines, trains, street noise) to about 1/8 of their original loudness" [7].

#### Apple AirPods Max (2nd Gen)
SoundGuys tests show the AirPods Max 2 reduces outside noise by 89.4%, compared to 88% for the original [10][11]. Apple claims "up to 1.5x more Active Noise Cancellation than the previous generation" thanks to the H2 chip [12][55]. CNET described the ANC as "arguably the best I've experienced, slightly edging out the noise canceling on both Sony's WH-1000XM4 and Bose's Noise Cancelling Headphones 700" [56]. RecordingNow gave the ANC a 10/10 rating, noting "nearly dead-silence with no noticeable hiss or white noise" [57]. SoundStage Global's measurements confirmed exceptional performance, especially in the 100Hz-1kHz "airplane band" [58].

#### Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless
The Momentum 4's ANC is notably weaker than its competitors. SoundGuys measured 76% noise attenuation, compared to 84-87% for the Sony XM5 and Bose QC Ultra [13][14]. TechGearLab rated ANC at 6.0/10, stating "the noise cancellation is where the Sennheiser struggles" [15]. The headphones use adaptive hybrid noise cancellation that automatically adapts to ambient noise [59]. While effective for home and office use, the ANC is not recommended for commuting or travel [15]. What Hi-Fi? noted that ANC is "very good but slightly behind Sony WH-1000XM5 in blocking loud noises" [60].

### 2. Sound Quality & Audio Accuracy

#### Bose QuietComfort 45
The QC45 has a treble-emphasized sound signature, with SoundGuys measuring a 5-8 dB boost in the 6-8 kHz region that makes some tracks sound harsh or sibilant [1]. RecordingNow described it as "the worst offender is the piercing treble, specifically around the 6-8 kHz region" [16]. A 3-band EQ was added via firmware update in February 2022, and SoundGuys recommends "dropping 3-20kHz by about 5dB" [1][17]. With EQ, the sound quality scores 8.0/10 [16]. The headphones support only SBC and AAC codecs, with no aptX, LDAC, or LE Audio support [1]. The 40mm dynamic drivers use TriPort acoustic architecture and Volume-optimized Active EQ [61].

#### Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen)
The 2nd Gen QC Ultra shares a similar sound signature to the 1st Gen, which SoundGuys described as "bass-forward" with CustomTune adaptive EQ [4]. SoundGuys noted that "the difference in projected sound quality ratings between generation 1 and generation 2 is not statistically significant" [4]. The 2nd Gen's tuning is "closer to our house curve" but "still somewhat bass heavy, though less so compared to the original" [18]. The headphones support Bluetooth 5.4 with SBC, AAC, and aptX Adaptive (including aptX Lossless), and add USB-C wired lossless audio (24-bit/48kHz) — a major new feature [4][19][52]. The 3-band EQ in the Bose Music app is limited, and reviewers recommend using custom EQ settings [19].

#### Sony WH-1000XM5
The XM5 has a bass-heavy, U-shaped frequency response. SoundGuys described it as having "decent sound quality with strong bass and treble emphasis" but with a recessed midrange [7]. HeadphonesAddict called the stock sound "muddy and dark" but said it becomes "natural and dynamic" after EQ adjustments [8]. RecordingNow noted that "the first thing that stuck out to me was how strange the stock sound signature was" but after applying custom EQ, "the resulting sound has so much more detail, airiness, and dynamics" [20]. The headphones support Bluetooth 5.2 with SBC, AAC, and LDAC codecs (LDAC does not work on iPhones) [7][21]. The 30mm drivers use carbon fiber composite diaphragm domes, reduced from 40mm in the XM4 [9][54]. MDAQS scores from SoundGuys: Timbre 4.3, Distortion 3.6, Immersiveness 4.1, Overall 4.2 [7].

#### Apple AirPods Max (2nd Gen)
The AirPods Max 2 has a neutral-bright sound signature. SoundGuys noted that "the measured frequency response for the AirPods Max follows our headphone preference curve fairly closely" [10]. Crinacle (In-Ear Fidelity) gave the original a Tone Grade of A- (overall Grade B-), describing it as a "very well-tuned headphone" with a "U-shaped" signature and "strong, well-controlled sub-bass" [22]. RecordingNow described the AirPods Max 2's sound as "neutral bright, 'clean', detailed, exciting, balanced, open soundstage, vocal-forward, with tight bass" with a sound quality rating of 8.8/10 [57]. The headphones support only SBC and AAC codecs, but the 2nd Gen adds 24-bit/48kHz lossless audio via USB-C wired connection [10][23]. Apple does not offer a native user EQ, though iOS 27 reportedly adds custom EQ for the AirPods Max 2 [62].

#### Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless
The Momentum 4 is widely considered the best-sounding among its ANC competitors. SoundGuys described the sound quality as "superior" and "ahead of the pack" [13]. Headphones.com rated it "the best-sounding ANC headphone among current competitors (Bose 700, QC45, WH-1000XM5, AirPods Max) for new buyers" [24]. The headphones have a "soft V-shape" with boosted sub-bass, dipped upper mids, and elevated treble [24]. What Hi-Fi? noted that the sound signature "has shed a layer or two of the richness that has characterised older Momentums, in favour of more neutrality this time round" [25]. The Momentum 4 supports Bluetooth 5.2 with SBC, AAC, aptX, aptX HD, and aptX Adaptive codecs [13][59]. The 42mm dynamic transducer system delivers Sennheiser's signature sound [59]. MDAQS scores: Timbre 4.8, Distortion 3.5, Immersiveness 4.1, Overall 4.6 [14].

### 3. Comfort and Build Materials

#### Bose QuietComfort 45
At 240 grams, the QC45 is among the lightest over-ear ANC headphones available [1][26]. SoundGuys described it as "one of the most comfortable headsets around" [1]. RecordingNow gave a comfort score of 9.5/10, noting that "the QC45 is the lightest headphones tested in its class" [16]. Crutchfield's reviewer stated: "I could wear them for entire work shifts without fatigue or strain on my head or ears" [27]. The headphones use plush synthetic leather (leatherette) over memory foam ear cushions, with a padded headband and glass-filled nylon frame [26][61]. The design is foldable with rotating earcups and comes with a hard-shell carrying case [1]. The build uses mostly impact-resistant nylon/plastic with custom cast-metal hinges (steel-reinforced) [51][61].

#### Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen)
The 2nd Gen QC Ultra weighs approximately 250 grams [4][28]. CNET described them as "the most comfortable pair of headphones you can buy right now" [63]. The headphones use protein leather (pleather) ear cushions with angled drivers and thick, plush ear cups [4][29]. The 2nd Gen features "refined materials" with a "polished metal headband" and "plush protein leather cushions" [52]. Mashable noted that the 2nd Gen has "ear foam that feels ever so slightly firmer while still offering a healthy amount of give" [18]. The design is foldable with a compact carry case [4]. The headphones have a "soft clamping force" that is glasses-friendly [64]. Both generations have no IP rating — no waterproofing or dust resistance [4]. SoundGuys noted "no waterproofing is a bit of a letdown" [4].

#### Sony WH-1000XM5
The XM5 weighs 250 grams but has a non-foldable design, which is a significant drawback for portability [7][30]. The headphones use "soft fit leather" (synthetic leather) ear cups with memory foam padding [7][31]. Crutchfield noted "roomier earcups with more padding and a more flexible headband" [65]. However, the build quality is predominantly plastic, which has drawn criticism. RecordingNow noted that "the biggest drawback is its questionable build quality around the hinges" and "the plastic hinges feel quite frail for a $400 headphone" [20]. A class action lawsuit (Kanter v. Sony, SDNY) is pending alleging that the XM5's swiveling earcup hinge contains a manufacturing defect [66]. TechGearLab rated comfort at 5.9/10, noting only 6 hours of comfortable wear [30]. Headphones.com reported "stronger clamp force" compared to the XM4 [24].

#### Apple AirPods Max (2nd Gen)
The AirPods Max 2 is significantly heavier than competitors at 386 grams [10][32]. The earcups are made from anodized aluminum, with a stainless steel headband frame and a breathable knit mesh canopy [10][33]. The ear cushions use memory foam with acoustically engineered mesh and are magnetically attachable and replaceable ($69 per pair) [33][55]. Comfort is a divisive issue. TechGearLab rated comfort at 4.6/10, stating: "The longer we wore the Max, the more we felt the weight on our head and neck, as well as the pressure on our ears" [32]. Brian Tong described them as "arguably the most uncomfortable over-the-ear headphones because over time the weight gets to you" [67]. However, RecordingNow gave a comfort rating of 8/10, noting the "mesh headband and large earcups" mitigate the weight somewhat [57]. The headphones have no power button — they require the Smart Case (or a magnet in the earcups) to enter ultra-low-power mode [10][33]. There is no IP rating [10].

#### Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless
The Momentum 4 weighs 293 grams, making it lighter than the AirPods Max but heavier than the Bose and Sony options [13][34]. The build is mostly plastic with metallic accents and a fabric-coated headband [24]. What Hi-Fi? rated comfort 5/5 [35]. TechGearLab gave a comfort score of 9.5/10, with a measured comfortable listening time of 10 hours [15]. However, RecordingNow noted that "the Momentum 4 has a lot of clamping force from the factory, along with a relatively stiff headband creating some top-of-head pressure" [34]. The headphones use deep cushioned synthetic leather earpads [59]. The design is foldable with a sturdy carrying case [24]. The ear cups are wider and pads softer than the Momentum 3, improving comfort [24]. The Guardian described the redesign as delivering "superior comfort" [68].

### 4. Battery Life

#### Bose QuietComfort 45
Bose originally claimed up to 24 hours with ANC on, though the official website now lists 22 hours [1][36]. SoundGuys tested 24 hours 49 minutes with ANC on [1]. Quick charging provides 15 minutes = 3 hours of playback, and full charge takes approximately 2.5 hours via USB-C [61]. The Bose official manual states a full charge time of ~2.5 hours [69]. Some users report that battery life can vary based on firmware versions and usage patterns [70].

#### Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen)
The 2nd Gen QC Ultra claims up to 30 hours with ANC on and Immersive Audio off, up to 23 hours with Immersive Audio on, and up to 45 hours with both ANC and Immersive Audio off [4][52]. SoundGuys tested 27 hours 12 minutes with ANC on [4][37]. Quick charging provides 15 minutes = 3 hours of playback, and full charge takes approximately 3 hours [52]. The 2nd Gen's battery life is a significant improvement over the 1st Gen, which tested at 27.6 hours with ANC on [4].

#### Sony WH-1000XM5
Sony claims 30 hours with ANC on and 40 hours with ANC off [7][38]. SoundGuys tested 31 hours 53 minutes with ANC on and 53 hours 6 minutes with ANC off [7]. HeadphonesAddict tested 40 hours 27 minutes with ANC on [8]. Quick charging is exceptional: 3 minutes provides 3 hours of playback with USB-PD compatible AC adapter, and 10 minutes provides 5 hours [7][9]. Full charge takes approximately 3.5 hours via USB-C [9][54]. However, battery life can be significantly reduced by using features like DSEE Extreme, Speak-to-Chat, EQ, and LDAC [7]. 2024 firmware updates reportedly increased battery drain by 7-10% [71].

#### Apple AirPods Max (2nd Gen)
Apple claims up to 20 hours of listening time with ANC or Transparency mode enabled [10][39]. SoundGuys tested the 2nd Gen at 21 hours 21 seconds, while the original tested at 22.8 hours [10][39]. Quick charging provides 5 minutes = 1.5 hours of listening time [10]. The battery life is the weakest aspect of the AirPods Max, with SoundGuys noting: "In 2026, that's downright disappointing — especially when flagship headphones from Sony and Sennheiser blow past the 35-hour mark on a single charge" [10]. The headphones require the Smart Case to enter ultra-low-power mode; otherwise, they continue to drain battery [10].

#### Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless
The Momentum 4 has exceptional battery life, with Sennheiser claiming up to 60 hours with ANC on [13][40]. SoundGuys tested 56 hours 21 minutes with ANC on, far exceeding all competitors [13]. What Hi-Fi? rated battery life 5/5, calling it "class-leading 60-hour battery life (double most rivals)" [35]. TechGearLab measured 49 hours (claimed 60 hours) [15]. Quick charging provides 6 hours of playback in 10 minutes, and full charge takes approximately 2 hours [59][72]. The 700 mAh lithium-polymer battery is the largest in this comparison [72].

## Summary and Recommendations

### By Priority

**Best ANC:** Bose QC Ultra (2nd Gen) and Sony WH-1000XM5 are tied for top honors, with the AirPods Max 2 close behind. The QC Ultra has a slight edge in mid-frequency attenuation, while the XM5 excels in overall consistency.

**Best Sound Quality:** Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless leads in pure audio fidelity, followed by the AirPods Max 2 (especially with USB-C lossless audio). The Bose QC Ultra and Sony XM5 require EQ adjustments to reach their full potential.

**Best Comfort:** Bose QC45 and QC Ultra are the most comfortable, with the Sennheiser Momentum 4 close behind. The AirPods Max 2 is the heaviest and most polarizing in comfort.

**Best Battery Life:** Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless dominates with 56+ hours tested, more than double the AirPods Max 2. The Sony XM5 is second with 31+ hours.

**Best Value:** Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless at $219-249 offers the best sound and battery life at a competitive price. The Sony WH-1000XM5 at $248 is also excellent value. The AirPods Max 2 at $399 (on sale) is premium-priced but offers unique Apple ecosystem integration.

### By Ecosystem

**Apple Ecosystem:** AirPods Max 2 is the clear choice for seamless integration with Apple devices, including automatic switching, spatial audio, and Siri.

**Android Ecosystem:** Sony WH-1000XM5 (with LDAC) and Sennheiser Momentum 4 (with aptX Adaptive) are excellent choices for Android users.

**Universal:** Bose QC Ultra (2nd Gen) offers the best balance of ANC, comfort, and features across platforms, while the Sennheiser Momentum 4 offers the best value and sound quality.
