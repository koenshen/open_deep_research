# AI/ML Medical Devices in Radiology with First-Ever FDA Clearance (August 5, 2024 – August 5, 2026)

This report lists all radiology AI/ML medical devices that received their first-ever FDA clearance (via 510(k) or De Novo) during the two-year period from August 5, 2024 to August 5, 2026. For each device, the device name, manufacturer, clearance date, a link to the official FDA AI/ML-enabled medical devices list entry, and a relevant med-tech news article are provided. The list includes devices from both established companies entering the FDA-regulated market for the first time and startup companies, spanning X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, and other radiology sub‑specialties.

- **Device Name:** a2z-Unified-Triage
- **Manufacturer:** a2z Radiology AI Inc. (Boston, MA)
- **Clearance Date:** November 24, 2025
- **FDA 510(k) Number:** K252366
- **FDA AI/ML Device List Entry:** [a2z-Unified-Triage 510(k) Summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf25/K252366.pdf)
