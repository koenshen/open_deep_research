# Top 25 Electric Vehicles (2026 Model Year) – Ranked by Composite Score

## Ranking Methodology

The following ranking is based on a composite score that equally weights three criteria: **EPA range (miles)**, **0–60 mph acceleration (seconds)**, and **starting MSRP (USD)**. Each criterion was normalized using min‑max scaling across all eligible vehicles, and the composite score is the arithmetic mean of the three normalized values. All data come from official manufacturer websites, EPA’s Fuel Economy database (fueleconomy.gov), and manufacturer press releases for the 2026 model year. For vehicles with multiple trims, the variant that maximizes the composite score (best balance of range, acceleration, and price) was selected. Only vehicles with official 2026 data are included.

## Analysis of the Ranking

### 1. Top Contenders – The Neue Klasse Revolution and Lucid’s Dominance
The **BMW iX3 50 xDrive** claims the #1 spot with a composite score of 0.7385, thanks to class‑leading EPA range of 434 miles, strong acceleration (4.7 sec 0–60), and a relatively accessible starting price of $61,500. As the first US‑market model on BMW’s Neue Klasse platform, it combines 800‑V architecture, 400‑kW charging, and an NACS port. Its range‑to‑price ratio is unmatched among 2026 EVs.

**Lucid Motors** occupies three of the top five positions. The **Lucid Air Touring** (431 mi, 3.4 sec, $79,900) scores 0.7252, offering the best acceleration‑for‑range trade‑off in the lineup. The **Lucid Air Pure** ($70,900, 420 mi, 4.5 sec) is a close fourth, and the **Lucid Gravity Grand Touring** (450 mi, 3.4 sec, $98,900) rounds out the top five. Lucid’s 900‑V architecture and industry‑leading efficiency allow it to dominate the range dimension while keeping acceleration competitive.

### 2. Tesla’s Strong Showing – Value and Performance Balance
Tesla places three models in the top 10. The **Model 3 Premium RWD** (#3) offers 363 miles of range at $42,490, making it one of the best‑value picks. The **Model Y Premium AWD** (#7) benefits from the Juniper refresh with improved ride quality and a 327‑mile range, while the **Cybertruck Dual Motor** (#17) lands lower due to its higher price and moderate range. Notably, Tesla’s Model S and Model X were discontinued in Q2 2026, so they are not included.

### 3. The Dark Horse – Rivian R2
The **Rivian R2 Performance AWD** (#8) is a strong newcomer, delivering 330 miles of range, a 3.6‑second 0–60, and a starting price of $57,990. Its composite score of 0.6216 is only 0.0001 behind the Model Y, making it one of the most balanced EVs on the market. The R2 uses native NACS charging and features Rivian’s latest software and driving dynamics.

### 4. Mainstream Value Leaders – Ford, Chevrolet, and Hyundai
The **Ford Mustang Mach‑E Premium** (#9) remains a compelling choice with 320 miles of range at $40,595. The **Chevrolet Equinox EV LT1** (#21) is the cheapest vehicle in the top 25 at $34,995 while still offering 319 miles of range, though its 7.7‑second 0–60 drags down its composite score. The **Hyundai Ioniq 5 SE Long Range** (#22) and **Kia EV6 Light Long Range** (#23) are near‑identical in specs and score, both offering 318–319 miles of range for around $40,000.

### 5. Luxury and Performance Highlights
The **Mercedes‑Benz CLA 250+** (#10) stands out for its 374‑mile range and relatively low starting price of $47,250, making it the most affordable long‑range luxury EV. The **Cadillac Lyriq Luxury** (#13) and **Volkswagen ID.4 Pro** (#14) offer solid range and acceleration but are outclassed by the top contenders. High‑performance models like the **Chevrolet Blazer EV SS** (#16) achieve 3.4‑second 0–60 but sacrifice range (283 mi) and carry a higher price.

### 6. Surprising Entries – Subaru Solterra and Nissan Leaf
The **Subaru Solterra Limited XT** (#12) is a surprise, ranking high due to its 4.3‑second 0–60 (the quickest Subaru ever tested) and a $42,895 price. Its 278‑mile range is the lowest in the top 25, but the acceleration and price compensate. The **Nissan Leaf S+** (#19) is the cheapest EV in the top 25 at $29,990, with 303 miles of range, making it the best value for budget‑conscious buyers.

### 7. Trucks and SUVs – GMC Sierra EV Leads
The **GMC Sierra EV Elevation Extended Range** (#6) is the top‑ranked truck, with 410 miles of range, 4.5‑second acceleration, and a $70,600 price. The **Tesla Cybertruck** (#17) trails due to its higher price and lower range. The **Rivian R1T** and **R1S Quad‑Motor** models (#24 and #25) offer blistering acceleration (2.6 sec) and high range (374 mi) but carry a $121,990 price tag, which significantly reduces their composite scores.

### 8. Omitted Models – Why Some Vehicles Didn’t Make the Cut
- **Lucid Air Grand Touring** (512 mi, 3.0 sec, $114,900): Despite having the longest range and excellent acceleration, its high price reduces the composite score to 0.3961, well outside the top 25.
- **Mercedes‑Benz EQS 450+** (390 mi, 5.9 sec, $99,900): Strong range but high price and slower acceleration yield a composite of 0.3961.
- **Porsche Macan Electric** (332 mi, 5.4 sec, $80,300): High price and moderate acceleration leave it at 0.4080.
- **Kia EV9** and **Hyundai Ioniq 9**: Both have ranges over 300 miles but slow acceleration (8.8 sec and 7.8 sec respectively) and relatively high prices.
- **BMW i3 Neue Klasse**: Not yet officially EPA‑certified for the US market as of August 2026; it is expected as a 2027 model.
- **Chinese‑brand EVs** (BYD, NIO, etc.): Not sold in the US due to tariffs and regulatory barriers; therefore excluded.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Range is the differentiator**: The top five vehicles all exceed 400 miles of EPA range, which dominates the composite score.
- **Price matters**: Affordable EVs like the Nissan Leaf and Chevrolet Equinox EV rank highly despite modest acceleration.
- **Acceleration can compensate**: The Blazer EV SS and Rivian Quad‑Motor models achieve high scores through superior 0–60 times, but their high prices or low range limit overall standing.
- **The 2026 market is diverse**: From the $29,990 Leaf to the $121,990 Rivian, the top 25 covers a wide spectrum of price and performance, offering choices for every buyer.
