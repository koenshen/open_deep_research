## Analysis and Observations

### Pattern of Spin-off Rebrands

A notable pattern emerges from the identified companies: **four out of six rebrands were spin-offs from larger parent companies** (Kenvue from J&J, Kellanova and WK Kellogg Co from Kellogg, Fortrea from LabCorp). This suggests that spin-off and restructuring events are a primary driver of corporate rebranding among S&P 500 consumer-facing companies during this period.

### Pharmaceutical and Healthcare Sector Dominance

Three of the six identified rebrands (Pfizer, Kenvue, Revvity) are in the pharmaceutical/healthcare space. This may reflect a broader trend of healthcare companies modernizing their visual identities post-pandemic, as consumer expectations for healthcare brands have evolved.

### Lack of Major Consumer Goods Rebrands

Notably, major consumer-facing companies in the food, beverage, retail, apparel, and technology sectors (Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, McDonald's, Nike, Apple, Walmart, Procter & Gamble, etc.) did **not** execute full corporate rebrands in this window. This suggests that the major consumer brands that dominated the news in prior years (e.g., Dunkin' in 2019, Mastercard in 2019, Meta in 2021) had already completed their rebranding cycles before 2023.
