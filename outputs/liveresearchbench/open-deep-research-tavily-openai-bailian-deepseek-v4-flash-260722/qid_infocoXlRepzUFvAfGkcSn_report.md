## How to Obtain the Required Information

To complete this research, the following steps are recommended:

1. **Access the Federal Reserve Board’s CRA Ratings Database** – The FRB publishes CRA performance evaluations for state member banks. Search for “CRA ratings” at [federalreserve.gov](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/cra_publications.htm). Look for evaluations with a rating of “Needs to Improve” or “Substantial Noncompliance.”
2. **Access the OCC’s CRA Performance Evaluations** – The OCC provides a searchable database of CRA evaluations for national banks. Visit [occ.gov](https://www.occ.gov/topics/consumers-and-communities/cra/index-cra-performance-evaluations.html) and filter by rating.
3. **Cross‑reference with news sources** – Once a bank is identified, search for “CRA rating needs to improve [bank name]” on news sites like American Banker, Reuters, Bloomberg, or The Wall Street Journal.
4. **Verify asset size** – Use the Federal Reserve’s quarterly call report data or the bank’s financial statements to confirm total assets exceed $50 billion.
