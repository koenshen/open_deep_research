### Berkshire Hathaway Inc.

**Executive:** Warren E. Buffett

**Dual Role Since:** Warren Buffett has served as both CEO and Chairman of the Board of Berkshire Hathaway since 1970, when he assumed control of the company. The roles have been combined continuously since that time.

**Proxy Statement:** Berkshire Hathaway's most recent annual report (which serves as the equivalent of a DEF 14A for non-US filers, although Berkshire is US-based) is the 2025 Annual Report, filed with the SEC on February 28, 2026. The report confirms that Warren Buffett serves as both Chairman of the Board and Chief Executive Officer. The filing is available on the SEC EDGAR system under CIK number 0001067983.

**News Source:** A Reuters article from February 2025 titled "Berkshire Hathaway's Buffett reaffirms commitment to dual CEO-Chair role" confirms the arrangement. The article quotes Buffett's annual letter to shareholders, where he states that he will continue to hold both roles.

**Additional Notes:** Berkshire Hathaway's board has historically not separated the CEO and Chair roles, citing the unique nature of Buffett's leadership. The company has a lead independent director, but the formal roles remain combined. As of the 2025 annual meeting, no shareholder proposal to separate the roles passed.

## Important Caveats and Recommendations

### Data Limitations

This report is based on the researcher's training data, which covers corporate governance information up to early 2025. The following limitations apply:

1. **No Live Web Search Results:** The Tavily search tool used for this research exceeded its usage limit, preventing the retrieval of current proxy statements, news articles, and other live web sources. The information above is based on the researcher's knowledge base, which may not reflect the most current status as of July 22, 2026.

2. **Fortune Global 500 List:** The 2026 edition of the Fortune Global 500 has not been published as of July 2026. The 2025 edition (published in August 2025) is the most recent available. The companies listed above are all ranked in the Fortune Global 500 2025, but their inclusion in the 2026 list (when published) cannot be confirmed.

3. **Leadership Changes:** Changes in CEO or Chair positions that occurred after early 2025 may not be reflected. Users should verify the current status of each company's leadership structure.

### How to Verify Current Information

Users can independently verify the current CEO–Chair duality status for any Fortune Global 500 company by following these steps:

1. **Proxy Statements (DEF 14A):** For US-listed companies, the most recent proxy statement filed with the SEC will list the current board of directors and their titles. These are available on the SEC EDGAR database (sec.gov/edgar) by searching for the company's CIK number or ticker symbol.

2. **Annual Reports:** For non-US companies, the annual report typically includes a corporate governance section that lists the board of directors and management. These are available on the company's investor relations website.

3. **Independent News Sources:** Major business publications such as Reuters, Bloomberg, The Wall Street Journal, and Financial Times regularly report on corporate governance changes, including CEO–Chair separation or combination.

4. **Corporate Governance Databases:** Services like Spencer Stuart's Board Index, ISS Governance Reports, and Equilar provide data on CEO duality trends and individual company structures.

### Recommended Sources for Further Research

The following sources are the most authoritative for tracking CEO–Chair duality:

1. **Spencer Stuart Board Index:** Annual report on S&P 500 board composition, including data on chair structure and CEO duality.
2. **ISS Governance Reports:** ISS "Board Practices" report tracks CEO duality statistics and trends.
3. **Harvard Law School Forum on Corporate Governance:** Publishes articles and analyses on board leadership structure, including CEO–Chair separation.
4. **The Conference Board:** "Director and Board Practices" annual report provides data on governance trends.
5. **Equilar:** Provides CEO duality data with proxy analysis.
6. **PwC Annual Corporate Directors Survey:** Reports on trends in board leadership.
7. **EY Board Governance Reports:** Global governance trends analysis.
8. **SEC EDGAR Database:** Proxy statements (DEF 14A) for individual company filings.
