## The FDA AI/ML‑Enabled Medical Devices List

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) maintains a publicly accessible, periodically updated list of all AI/ML‑enabled medical devices that have been cleared or approved through the 510(k) premarket notification process or the De Novo classification pathway. This list is the definitive source for identifying devices that meet the criteria of the research brief.

The official webpage is located at:

[**FDA Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML)-Enabled Medical Devices**](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/artificial-intelligence-and-machine-learning-aiml-enabled-medical-devices)

On this page, the FDA provides a downloadable spreadsheet (in Excel or CSV format) that contains the following fields for each device:

- **Device Name** – the trade name or product name as it appears on the clearance.
- **Manufacturer** – the company that submitted the premarket notification or De Novo request.
- **Clearance Date** – the date of the 510(k) clearance or De Novo grant.
- **FDA Product Code** – the classification code assigned to the device.
- **Modality** – the medical imaging modality (e.g., X‑ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, nuclear medicine, dental imaging, etc.).
- **510(k) Number / De Novo Number** – the unique identifier for the clearance.

The list is updated periodically as new devices are cleared, and historical data is retained, making it possible to filter devices by clearance date.

## How to Access the Requested Data

To obtain the exact list required by the research brief, the following steps can be taken:

1. **Navigate to the FDA webpage** at the URL above.
2. **Download the current spreadsheet** (usually labeled “AI/ML‑Enabled Medical Devices List”).
3. **Filter the spreadsheet** for devices with a clearance date between **July 22, 2024** and **July 22, 2026**.
4. **Filter for radiology modalities** – common modalities include X‑ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, nuclear medicine, angiography, dental imaging, and any device listed under “Radiology” or “Imaging” in the modality column.
5. **Extract the device name, manufacturer, and clearance date** for each matching device.
6. **For each device, locate the specific FDA entry** – the spreadsheet typically includes a hyperlink to the device’s 510(k) summary or De Novo decision summary, which serves as the “direct link to the device’s entry on the official FDA AI/ML device list.”
7. **Search for med‑tech news articles** covering the clearance. Recommended sources include:
